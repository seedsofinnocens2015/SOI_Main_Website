What Is an IVF Doctor in Gurgaon?

An IVF doctor is a fertility specialist who can diagnose and treat medical problems that might interfere with conception. They may help their patients with various fertility problems, either female or male, and advise on treatments such as ovulation induction, IUI, or IVF, depending on the situation.

The best IVF doctors in Gurgaon may also address fertility issues caused by PCOS, endometriosis, blocked fallopian tubes, low ovarian reserve, male infertility, unexplained infertility, and age-related fertility decline.

IVF may be one of the options for treatment; however, the most appropriate treatment depends on the specific situation. It should be chosen for each couple individually and does not always include IVF, even if other treatments are not effective.

When Should You See a Fertility or IVF Specialist?

Some couples take years before visiting a fertility expert. Visiting a specialist sooner could help in identifying the cause and treatment options.

Typically, couples could opt to have a fertility workup after 12 months of regular intercourse without conceiving when the female partner is below 35 years.

Where the woman is 35 years and above, a fertility workup is normally recommended after six months of attempting. Early fertility workup may be indicated if the couple has fertility issues, irregular periods, prior pelvic surgery, recurrent miscarriage, and other fertility problems.

If you have been diagnosed with a fertility issue before, there is no need for you to wait.

A discussion with an infertility expert in Gurgaon could help decide what your next move should be.

What Can an IVF Doctor Treat?

An infertility expert can diagnose and treat several reasons behind infertility, such as:

  • PCOS and ovulation disorders
  • Ovarian reserve deficiency
  • Damaged or blocked fallopian tubes
  • Endometriosis
  • Fibroids and other uterine problems
  • Male-factor infertility
  • Low sperm count or motility
  • Failed fertility treatments in the past
  • Unexplained infertility
  • Age-related infertility issues
  • It all depends on the diagnosis.

In some cases, just changing the lifestyle and taking some drugs might help.

Other Cases Will Need IUI or IVF.

When looking for the best IVF doctor in Gurgaon, people tend to focus only on the success rates of clinics.

However, IVF results may differ greatly from one person to another.

Age, egg and sperm quality, embryo development, general reproductive health, and the cause of infertility can all affect treatment results.

So, do not believe the claims of some clinics or advertisements regarding guaranteed pregnancy or a 100% success rate of IVF treatment.

Rather, have the doctor explain to you your own chances, potential problems, and realistic treatment expectations.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which Is the Best IVF Doctor in Gurgaon?

There is no one best IVF doctor in Gurgaon for all patients. The best IVF doctor in Gurgaon is the one who suits your diagnosis, age, fertility history, treatment requirements, and personal choices. You should seek qualified doctors with fertility experience, clear communication, and a clear treatment process.

2. What Does an IVF Specialist Do?

An IVF specialist diagnoses fertility issues and suggests the appropriate treatment, such as medicines, IUI, or IVF.

3. Is IVF the First Option for Infertility Treatment?

No, not necessarily, For some causes of infertility, medications, lifestyle modifications, ovulation induction, or IUI are prescribed before IVF. If the couple is clinically suitable for IVF, then it is advised.

4. How Can I Find the Best Fertility Doctor in Gurgaon?

Check the credentials, experience with fertility treatments, treatment method used by the doctor, clinic facility, patient interaction, and transparency regarding costs and expectations. A consultation will help you make up your mind regarding the doctor.

5. When Should I Consult a Fertility Specialist?

If you have been trying to conceive without any luck, consulting a fertility specialist may be necessary if you have been trying for one year if the woman is below 35 years or six months if she is 35 years or older.
