Qualifications of IVF Doctors in India

The following is a set of qualifications and specializations that are certainly needed for IVF doctors in India:

Medical Degree: A fertiliy expert should have a medical degree (M.D. or D.O.) from a recognized university.

Residency Training: After graduating from the medical school, doctors complete the residency in obstetrics and gynaecology to gain expertise in women’s health.

Fellowship Training: After completing their residency, they receive fellowship training in reproductive endocrinology and infertility. This specialised education is well-geared towards identifying and treating reproductive issues.
