Who Is an IVF Doctor in Kasaragod?

An IVF doctor is a specialist who deals with fertility problems and helps individuals and couples achieve conception. The doctor analyzes the reason for infertility and suggests the appropriate treatment according to age, ovarian function, sperm health, medical history, past pregnancies, and earlier fertility treatments. The best fertility doctor in Kasaragod might suggest the following treatments as per their judgment:

  • IUI
  • IVF
  • ICSI
  • Ovulation induction
  • Fertility preservation methods
  • Embryo cryopreservation
  • Treatment for male factor infertility
  • Treatments for recurrent pregnancy losses

But IVF is not the first treatment that an IVF specialist will suggest. A good fertility specialist in Kasaragod first finds out the cause of infertility and then decides whether simpler treatment options can work.

Some of the conditions that an infertility specialist in Kasaragod may look into include:

  • Unexplained infertility

The first step would involve a thorough medical history and fertility examination. Your physician may order tests such as blood tests, ultrasound, semen analysis, among others, depending on the case.

When Should You See a Fertility Specialist?

Most couples will take several years before seeking professional advice. Early evaluation can at times detect fertility issues which can be treated.

In general, couples should seek a fertility consultation when they have not conceived after 12 months of regular intercourse. If the female partner is above 35 years, fertility evaluation is usually recommended after six months of trying.

It would also be wise to consult a doctor for infertility in Kasaragod earlier if you have any of the following: a fertility problem, irregular ovulation, previous miscarriages, previous pelvic surgery, tubal factor, or male factor infertility.

The main thing to remember is that there is no need to wait for a year if there is a reason to visit a doctor.

What Happens at Your First Consultation for IVF?

At your first visit, you will discuss your fertility background and possible causes of your infertility.

Your IVF doctor in Kasaragod will ask you about:

  • Your menstrual cycle
  • Previous pregnancies
  • Previous miscarriages
  • Previous fertility treatment
  • Medical and surgical history
  • Family history
  • Lifestyle issues
  • Reproductive health of your partner

Your doctor will then advise you on what tests you should take for both you and your partner.

This is important since infertility can affect one or both partners.
