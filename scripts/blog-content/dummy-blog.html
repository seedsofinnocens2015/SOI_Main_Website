What Fertility Treatments Can You Get in Ghaziabad?

Depending on the centre and the medical requirements of the patient, fertility treatment may consist of several treatments.

Ovulation Induction

Medicines can be prescribed to help induce ovulation in women with irregular or no ovulation at all.

IUI

Intrauterine insemination is a process in which prepared sperm is placed in the uterus around the time of ovulation. It may suit some specific cases of infertility.

IVF

In vitro fertilization involves inducing ovaries, egg retrieval, fertilization in the lab and transfer of the embryo in the uterus.

ICSI

It is the process of injecting a single sperm into an egg. It may be advised in some cases, especially those where there are certain male-factor infertility issues or past fertilization problems.

Embryo Freezing

Depending on the treatment protocol, suitable embryos can be frozen for future use.

Fertility Preservation

Freezing eggs or sperms is a consideration in case of people who want to ensure their future fertility needs are met. You can discuss the possible options with your fertility specialist in Ghaziabad depending on your fertility profile.
