What is Hysteroscopy?

Hysteroscopy is a process where a thin tube, known as hysteroscope, is inserted through the vagina and cervix into the uterus. The hysteroscope contains a camera and a light source enabling the surgeon to visualize the inner lining of the uterine cavity.

Unlike conventional surgery, hysteroscopy does not involve making an incision on the abdomen. It may be done either for diagnosis or treatment purposes.

Types of Hysteroscopy:

There are mainly two types of hysteroscopy.

Diagnostic Hysteroscopy

Diagnostic hysteroscopy is done to check the uterus for any potential abnormalities. It is recommended when a patient has any of the following problems:

  • Unusual or excessive menstrual bleeding
  • Irregular menstruation
  • Recurring miscarriages
  • Problems getting pregnant
  • Suspected uterine polyps
  • Suspected presence of fibroids within the uterus
  • Uterine adhesions or scar tissue
  • Congenital deformities of the uterus
  • The cost of the hysteroscopy test in Delhi is dependent on several factors.

Operative Hysteroscopy

An operative hysteroscopy is done in case the doctor has to treat a particular condition that exists within the process. In some cases, the surgeon may remove uterine polyps, certain types of fibroids, and adhesions.

Since operative procedures might require special equipment, anaesthesia, an operating room, and more skilled surgeons, the hysteroscopy price in Delhi is higher for an operative hysteroscopy as compared to diagnostic procedures.

When Is Hysteroscopy Recommended?

It might happen that your healthcare provider will recommend you this procedure in case other procedures cannot identify what is going on in the uterus.

The hysteroscopy process could help diagnose or treat:

Uterine Polyps

These are abnormal growths found in the mucous membrane layer of the uterus. They depend on the size and position of the polyp to cause abnormal bleeding or fertility problems.

Submucosal Fibroids

Some fibroids grow into the cavity of the uterus causing heavy bleeding, pain, and reproductive problems. Hysteroscopic surgery could remove some selected fibroids without opening the abdomen.

Uterine Adhesions

Scar tissue inside the uterus could affect the period or fertility. Hysteroscopy makes it possible to examine and treat certain adhesions.

Abnormal Uterine Bleeding

Patients who have unexplained or persistent abnormal uterine bleeding may require hysteroscopy in order to determine any abnormalities within the uterus.

Fertility and Reproductive Issues

In some cases, women being evaluated for fertility issues may require hysteroscopy in order to diagnose uterine problems that can impact conception or pregnancy.

Advantages of Hysteroscopy

There are certain benefits to hysteroscopy over other surgical methods that involve larger incisions in the body.

Some of these benefits can include:

  • No large abdominal cut
  • Direct viewing of the uterus
  • Diagnosis and therapy can often be done in one surgery
  • Little to no scarring
  • Faster recovery time in many cases
  • Hospital stay can be shorter for appropriate patients
  • Increased accuracy in treatment of certain uterine problems

However, not all women are appropriate for hysteroscopy. Consulting an expert specialist is necessary prior to treatment.
