What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI means Intrauterine Insemination. This is a procedure where specially processed sperm is inserted into the uterus of a woman close to the time when an egg is released by her ovary. IUI helps get healthy sperm close to the egg to help fertilisation. The process itself is fast and doesn't involve surgery or admission into the hospital. Depending on your medical background, age, sperm quality, and other factors, your fertility doctor might suggest IUI.

What Factors Influence the IUI treatment cost in India?

Every couple doesn't necessarily have to go through the same treatment process. While some couples may need only basic monitoring, others may require fertility medicines and other tests.

The main factors that influence the IUI treatment cost in Haldwani include:

1. Fertility medicines: Some women may need to take medicines to help develop and release eggs.

2. Ultrasound monitoring: Doctors monitor follicle growth to determine the right time for IUI.

3. Semen preparation: The semen is prepared to choose the right sperm for insemination.

4. Investigations: Some tests like blood and hormone tests may be required.

5. Number of cycles: Some couples may need more than one IUI cycle to conceive.

Knowing all these factors will help couples prepare for the treatment costs.

IUI VS IVF: Which Fertility Treatment Is Better?

Many couples are looking for IVF prices in Haldwani and also wish to know the difference between IUI and IVF. Both are fertility treatments, but there are differences in procedures.

IUI Treatment:

  • Insemination of sperm into the uterus.
  • Fertilization takes place naturally in the body.
  • It is relatively non-invasive.
  • Less expensive compared to IVF.

IVF Treatment:

  • Collection of eggs from the ovaries.
  • Eggs and sperm are mixed in the laboratory.
  • The embryo is transferred into the uterus.
  • More medical procedures and laboratory work are involved.
  • IUI can be a good first choice for selected couples, while IVF is recommended when IUI will not be helpful or when other fertility issues exist.
