What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI is the abbreviation used for Intrauterine Insemination, which is a fertility treatment procedure whereby the prepared sperm is inserted into the uterus around the period of ovulation. The procedure aims to bring healthy sperm close to the egg to enhance fertilisation. Unlike IVF, IUI is a less complicated and affordable treatment procedure. However, IUI is not appropriate for all couples. Your fertility doctor may recommend the IUI treatment method depending on various factors, including age, ovulation, sperm quality, fallopian tube status, and infertility duration.

What Is Included in an IUI Cycle?

An IUI cycle may include several procedures:

1. Fertility Assessment

First of all, your doctor will review your medical and fertility history. Tests will be recommended to check your ovulation, ovarian reserve, sperm quality, and other things that may influence your ability to conceive.

2. Ovulation Monitoring

Your doctor can check your follicle growth through ultrasounds. Sometimes, medication will be prescribed to support your IUI cycle.

3. Sperm Preparation

A semen specimen will be processed in the laboratory on the day of IUI to isolate and prepare healthy and motile sperm.

4. IUI Procedure

Sperm are introduced into the uterus through a catheter. It usually takes only a few minutes.

5. Pregnancy Test

A pregnancy test is conducted two weeks after IUI to see if pregnancy occurs.

IUI vs IVF? Which Treatment Would Suit You?

While looking for IUI cost in Ghaziabad, many people look up for cost of IVF in Ghaziabad as well as cost of IVF in Ghaziabad. Though these are both assisted fertility techniques, they are two very different procedures. The procedure of IUI involves inserting sperm into the uterus while fertilisation happens naturally inside the body. On the other hand, IVF treatment involves ovarian stimulation, collection of eggs, and fertilisation done in the laboratory, with the help of an embryo being placed into the uterus. Typically, the cost of the IVF procedure in Ghaziabad lies somewhere between ₹1.15 lakh and ₹2.4 lakh or higher, depending on medications and other services used in the IVF procedure. Hence, everyone does not need to go for the next step, which is IVF treatment.

There is no definite number for all couples. Your fertility doctor will assess the response after every cycle and determine whether IUI should continue or some other treatment should be pursued.
