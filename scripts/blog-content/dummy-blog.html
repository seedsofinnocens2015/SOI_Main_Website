The cost of the treatment is one of the major considerations for couples opting for infertility treatment. The cost of IVF in Gorakhpur may vary based on a number of reasons such as the treatment cycle, diagnostic tests, medications, lab charges, and the number of IVF cycles needed. IVF is a procedure through which the eggs are harvested from the ovaries and fertilised by the sperm in a laboratory. The fertilized egg, i.e., the embryo, is then put inside the uterus. Since there is no standard need for fertility for all couples, the cost of treatment varies accordingly. This guide provides details of the different elements of IVF treatment cost in Gorakhpur.

What is IVF Cost in Gorakhpur?

In general, the cost of one standard cycle of IVF in Gorakhpur is estimated to be ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,50,000 or even more based on various aspects such as clinic, treatment package, medicines, investigation tests, and other additional treatments needed.

But it must be noted that the actual IVF cost in Gorakhpur cannot be calculated only by the cost of the base treatment package, as some clinics offer the IVF procedure as an individual service, whereas medicines, investigations, embryo freezing, and other laboratory procedures are quoted as separate services.

So, it would be wise for couples to have a detailed cost quote from the fertility center before beginning the treatment.

What is Usually Included in IVF Treatment Cost?

Understanding the inclusion list of an IVF package helps couples plan their budget better. The cost of an IVF treatment in Gorakhpur might consist of various phases.

1. Initial consultation and fertility assessment

The first phase usually comprises a consultation with the fertility specialist. Depending on the health history of the patient, the doctor may advise conducting various blood tests, ultrasound scanning, semen analysis, hormonal testing, and other fertility investigations.

2. Ovarian Stimulation

In an IVF cycle, one might be given injectable fertility drugs in order to stimulate the ovaries to produce several follicles. Dosage and duration vary for different people.

Thus, the cost of the drugs may add considerably to the total cost of IVF in Gorakhpur.

3. Egg Retrieval

Once the follicles have matured, the eggs are retrieved via a process known as oocyte retrieval.

This procedure is usually carried out under medical supervision and sometimes under anaesthesia/sedation.

4. Sperm Collection and Fertilization

The laboratory fertilizes eggs with sperm after retrieval. Depending on the fertility issues faced by the couple, conventional IVF or intracytoplasmic sperm injection may be used.

The latter process may cost more than the former process.

5. Embryo Culture and Embryo Transfer

After fertilization, embryos will be cultured in the laboratory for a certain period. A suitable embryo will then be transferred to the uterus.

Sometimes extra embryos may be present; in this case, the couple can opt for cryopreservation.

6. Pregnancy Test and Follow-Up

Following the embryo transfer, the fertility doctor will typically recommend a pregnancy test after a suitable period of time. Follow-up visits and medication may also be included in the total cost of the treatment process.

Factors Influencing IVF Cost in Gorakhpur

Some factors which may affect the final cost of IVF in Gorakhpur include:

  • Age of the woman
  • Ovarian reserve
  • Nature and length of infertility
  • Quality of sperm
  • Medicines for fertility
  • Type of IVF/ICSI treatment
  • Number of treatment cycles needed
  • Embryo cryopreservation
  • Other diagnostic tests
  • Use of donor eggs/sperms if needed
  • Genetic screening of embryos if recommended
  • Other fertility treatments suggested by the fertility doctor

As the above factors vary from one person to another, simply considering the advertised package cost will not give you a clear idea of your total costs.

Are IVF and Test Tube Baby Treatments the same?

IVF and test tube baby treatment are often considered synonymous terms. Test tube baby treatment cost in Gorakhpur pertains to the cost of IVF treatment. However, though popularly known as the test tube baby treatment, fertilization doesn't occur in the test tube at all. In a test tube baby procedure, fertilization and growth of embryos take place in a specialized laboratory setting, and then the embryo is transferred to the uterus. Treatment costs depend on the specific course prescribed by your fertility expert.

Tips on Managing IVF Costs

IVF can be expensive. Before starting IVF treatment, one can inquire from the clinic about how the treatment will be performed and how much the costs will be.

One can also ask about the additional costs that might be incurred in case other techniques like ICSI, freezing of the embryos, embryo transfer after freezing, or genetic screening are recommended.

Instead of going to a particular clinic because of lower initial costs, it is better to know what is included in the package and if there are any additional costs excluded.
