When Should You See an Infertility Doctor in Meerut?

You do not need to see an IVF specialist immediately after failing to conceive.

You may think about having a fertility check-up if:

  • You have been trying to conceive for almost a year if you are less than 35 years old.
  • You have been trying for half a year if you are more than 35 years old.
  • You have irregular or no menstrual cycles.
  • You have PCOS, endometriosis, or any other reproductive disorders.
  • You have had many miscarriages.
  • You have blocked/damaged fallopian tubes.
  • Your partner has a low sperm count, poor sperm motility, or any other semen disorder.
  • You have undergone some other infertility treatments in the past and failed.

What Are the Types of Fertility Treatments in Meerut?

There might be several treatments for you to consider based on the type of diagnosis. Some of these are as follows.

1. IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)

Involves insertion of sperm into the uterus after being prepared.

2. IVF (In Vitro Fertilization)

It involves the collection of eggs, which are fertilized with the help of sperm. An embryo will be created in the process and will be put inside the uterus.

3. ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)

It involves the direct injection of a single sperm into the egg. It might particularly come in handy for male infertility problems.

Other fertility treatments include ovulation treatment, fertility treatment, embryo freezing, sperm/egg freezing, among others.
