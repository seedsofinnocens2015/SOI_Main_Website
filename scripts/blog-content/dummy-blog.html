What is IVF treatment?

In Vitro Fertilisation (IVF) is one type of assisted reproductive technology that could help couples who are having trouble conceiving naturally.

To put it in simple terms, eggs are harvested from the female and then fertilised by sperm in a laboratory setting. Post-fertilisation, the embryo formed is placed in the uterus.

IVF can be done in cases where there is tubal blockage, some male factor infertility issues, ovulation problems, decreased ovarian reserve, endometriosis, unexplained infertility, or failure of other fertility treatment methods. IVF treatment is preceded by a consultation with a fertility expert for both partners.

What Determines the IVF Procedure Cost in Guwahati?

The IVF fertility procedure in Guwahati can have variable cost among other people depending on several things that determine the overall cost of the treatment.

1. The Age of the Woman

The age of the woman can affect her ovarian reserve and egg quality as well as the type of treatment to be recommended.

2. The Type of Fertility Problem

Problems such as blocked tubes, low ovarian reserve, endometriosis, PCOS and male-factor infertility can require various treatments.

3. Medications

The medications used for ovarian stimulation will also contribute to the overall cost of IVF.

4. IVF Procedure

There is a difference between standard IVF and IVF with ICSI procedures in terms of cost.

5. Embryo Freezing/Storage

Embryos that need to be frozen may attract further costs in the process.
