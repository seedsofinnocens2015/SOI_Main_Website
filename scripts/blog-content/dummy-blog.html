Pregnancy is associated with numerous changes in the female body, one of which is vaginal discharge. Women frequently report increased vaginal discharge during pregnancy and question whether this phenomenon should be considered a natural physiological process. Knowledge about different types of discharge can help women deal with their symptoms and seek help if needed. This blog discusses about vaginal discharge during pregnancy, reasons for its occurrence, its types, warning signs, and advice on how to take care of your vagina.

What Is Vaginal Discharge in Pregnancy?

Discharge is the product of glands in the vagina and cervix. During pregnancy, changes in hormones and increased blood supply to the pelvic area often lead to increased discharge. The main function of yellow discharge during pregnancy is to keep the vagina free from any infections and prevent bacteria from reaching the fetus.

Normal vaginal discharge during pregnancy is called leukorrhea. It is usually thin and either clear or slightly white.

Why is There More Vaginal Discharge While Pregnant?

Some reasons contribute to an increased vaginal discharge during pregnancy:

  • 1. Hormonal Shifts

    • Hormones in pregnant women rise in levels; in particular, there is an estrogen hormone surge during pregnancy, which stimulates the production of fluids by the vaginal glands.

  • 2. Higher Amounts of Blood

    • During pregnancy, the body pumps more blood into the pelvic region. This encourages the vaginal membranes to secrete more fluids.

  • 3. Prevention of Infections

    • Increased amounts of discharge act as a cleansing mechanism that removes bacteria and other microorganisms and protects against vaginal infections.

  • 4. Cervical Modifications

    • As pregnancy progresses, the cervix also goes through some modifications; the cervix is known to secrete mucus as part of its function, which contributes to the increase in discharge during pregnancy.

    Meaning of Various Types of Vaginal Discharges

    White Discharge during pregnancy

    This type of vaginal discharge in pregnancy usually occurs in the second and third trimesters and is characterized by being white in color.

    Clear and Watery Discharge

    It is a form of vaginal discharge that is clear in color and very watery.

    Mucus-Like Discharge

    Usually, a mucus-like vaginal discharge signifies the expulsion of the mucus plug.

    Yellow or Green Discharge

    If your discharge during pregnancy is yellow, green, or gray, it could be caused by an infection, such as a sexually transmitted disease (STD) or bacterial infection. You should see a doctor for a proper examination.

    Brown Discharge

    If the discharge is brown, it usually consists of old blood. While it may happen after intercourse or examination of the vagina, prolonged brown discharge should be investigated.

    Pink Discharge

    If you have pink-colored discharge, it may be because of light bleeding, cervical irritation, and other changes associated with labor. You should discuss this issue with your physician, especially if there is cramping accompanying the discharge.

    When Is Vaginal Discharge a Red Flag?

    While discharge from the vagina often signifies nothing serious, some changes may signal problems. Call your health care professional in case:

  • 1. There is a Strong or Unpleasant Smell

    • The foul or fishy smell may show that there is an infection, such as bacterial vaginosis.

  • 2. You Feel Itchy or Burn

    • It may be a symptom of a yeast infection or another vaginal problem requiring medical attention.

  • 3. The Discharge Is Yellow, Green, or Gray

    • These changes in color often point to infection and need a prompt checkup.

  • 4. Sudden Gush of Fluid

    • A continuous trickle or sudden gush of clear fluid could indicate leaking amniotic fluid and should be assessed by a healthcare professional immediately.

  • 5. Pelvic Pain or Fever

    • Discharge accompanied by fever, abdominal pain, or pelvic discomfort may indicate an infection that requires medical care.

    Is Vaginal Discharge a Sign of Labor?

    For those women who are close to their delivery time, they can experience vaginal discharge along with some mucus or traces of blood, which is known as the "bloody show." This means that the dilation of the cervix occurs, and delivery will soon happen either within days or weeks. Not every woman experiences this phenomenon because the onset of labor doesn't necessarily require changes in vaginal discharge.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. Is vaginal discharge common in pregnancy?

    It is quite common to experience vaginal discharge in pregnancy due to hormonal changes and increased circulation.

    2. What does the color of pregnancy discharge look like?

    The color of normal pregnancy discharge can vary from clear, white, or even milky without any smell.

    3. When should I be concerned about vaginal discharge?

    If the color is yellowish, greenish, grayish, there's an unpleasant smell, presence of itching, burning, pain, or even blood, then you should get in touch with your doctor right away.

    4. Can vaginal discharge be one of the first signs of pregnancy?

    Yes, it can be, as the hormonal levels rise, which leads to vaginal discharge becoming more frequent.

    5. What's the difference between vaginal discharge and amniotic fluid leakage?

    Vaginal discharge will be thicker, whereas amniotic fluid will have a clear, waterlike appearance. Amniotic fluid leakage can be constant, and you need to contact a physician right away if you suspect that this has happened.
