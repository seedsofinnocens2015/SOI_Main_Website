One-stop-shop for Assisted Reproductive Services

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

This is among the most successful treatments offered for infertility caused by blocked fallopian tubes, ovarian reserve issues, unexplained infertility, endometriosis, or male factor infertility.

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)

ICSI is an advanced form of treatment that is carried out when there is poor quality and quantity of sperms that affect natural fertilization. The procedure involves injecting a sperm into the egg.

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

The procedure is done if there is need to treat certain fertility problems and it is normally the case prior to IVF treatment.

Fertility Preservation

Men and women who intend to preserve their fertility potential in the future may take advantage of the fertility preservation service.

Egg and Embryo Freezing

Freezing of eggs or embryo through advanced freezing technology makes it possible to save them for future use in IVF treatment.

Donor Program

There are donor egg and sperm programs available for couples requiring donor sperms or eggs.

1. Will IVF work for me?

IVF is not equally effective for everyone. Age, infertility diagnosis, quality of your embryos and reproductive system condition affect the success rate of the treatment. Individual treatment plan will improve your chances.

2. Who is a candidate for IVF?

IVF can be suggested to couples with problems like blocked fallopian tubes, male infertility, reduced ovarian reserve, recurrent failed IVF cycles, endometriosis, unexplained infertility, advanced maternal age and other conditions.
