What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI means intrauterine insemination. It is a fertility procedure that involves placing specially prepared sperm inside the woman's uterus at the time of ovulation. IUI is a relatively easy process that does not involve any surgeries or stays at the hospital. In accordance with the patient’s situation, your doctor will prescribe either natural cycle IUI or medicines for ovulation. Before starting treatment, a fertility expert assesses the reproductive system of both partners to find out if IUI is right for you.

Which Factors Influence IUI Treatment Cost in Patna?

The cost of IUI treatment in Patna can be different for each couple. The major influencing factors include:

Who Should Get IUI Treatment?

IUI may be helpful for certain couples suffering from:

  • Mild infertility issues on the side of a man.
  • Issues with ovulation, in some instances.
  • Unexplained infertility after proper assessment.
  • Certain circumstances in which the use of donor sperm is necessary.

IUI may not suit everybody. For instance, if there are blocked fallopian tubes or other certain fertility issues, then something else is required.

Your fertility doctor can go through your test results, offer you the options you have and choose what would fit you best.

IUI Treatment and IVF: What Is the Difference?

Both IUI and IVF help infertile people conceive, but both procedures are different. During IUI, the prepared sperm is placed into the uterus, and the fertilisation process occurs in the body. During IVF, eggs are extracted from the body, fertilised by sperm in the laboratory, and placed back into the uterus. IUI is a cheaper and less invasive procedure compared to IVF. The appropriate therapy depends on a range of factors: causes of infertility, age, test results, etc.

2. What is the cost of IUI in India?

The cost of IUI in India differs according to city, hospital, and requirements. It may cost several thousand rupees to even more than ₹20,000 per cycle. Make sure that you always ask for a personalised quotation at your selected clinic.

3. Is IUI less expensive compared to IVF?

Yes, IUI is less expensive in comparison with IVF since in the latter one egg retrieval and fertilization in the laboratory take place. But multiple attempts with IUI may also make it very expensive.

4. How many IUI cycles should I have to conceive?

There is no certain number. Some couples conceive in the first cycle, while some do not conceive via IUI. Your doctor will analyze your age, diagnosis and results before recommending to switch over to some other procedure or to try again.

Each clinic provides different packages that may contain insemination procedures, sperm preparation, consultations, or medicines. Ensure about all the inclusions before paying for your treatment.
