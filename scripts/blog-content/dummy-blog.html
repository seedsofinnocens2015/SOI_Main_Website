What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI, or Intrauterine Insemination, is a type of fertility treatment that aims to facilitate a higher probability of sperm reaching the egg.

A semen sample is selected in a laboratory in order to choose healthier sperm with better motility. Then, the selected sperm is transferred to the woman’s uterus via a tube. This procedure is normally carried out at the time of the woman's ovulation.

With IUI, there is no fertilization of the egg outside the reproductive tract. It will happen naturally.

Doctors can advise on the use of IUI in case of some specific types of infertility, for instance, male factor infertility, ovulation disorders, or use of donor sperm.

There are cases where IUI will not be recommended to you. Your doctor may recommend taking some tests first.

During your initial consultation, your fertility specialist will conduct an overview of your medical background, past pregnancies, menstruation cycles and fertility results.

2. Fertility Testing

Depending upon your circumstances, your doctor can suggest tests like semen analysis, ultrasound testing, hormonal tests, or any other test.

Ovulation can be monitored through ultrasound scan.

5. Sperm Preparation

The sperm is chosen from the semen sample before insemination.

6. IUI Procedure

Sperm is then introduced to the uterus through a thin catheter. The process is short and usually does not require you to stay at the hospital.

IUI or IVF? Which Treatment Is More Expensive?

Patients frequently face the dilemma of what procedure to select – IUI or IVF.

Typically, the cost of IUI treatment in India is much lower than that of IVF since IUI procedure does not require egg retrieval, fertilisation in a laboratory, embryo cultivation, and embryo transfer.

Charges for IVF in Gurugram will be much higher as IVF implies several extra steps.

The same way, the cost of IVF in Gurugram will vary depending on medication, stimulation protocol, laboratory work, ICSI, embryo cryopreservation, genetic testing, etc.

Hence, IUI can be viewed as an economical approach to fertility treatment for eligible patients. Nevertheless, going by price alone is not recommended when selecting a type of treatment. Your physician should know which form of treatment will give you a chance according to your fertility assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the average cost of IUI in Gurugram?

Average cost of IUI in Gurugram is about ₹8,000 – ₹25,000 but the cost may vary based on the facilities such as medicines, tests and monitoring that might be covered in your package.

2. What facilities are included in IUI treatment cost?

Depending on the clinic that you have chosen, the cost of treatment may include the process of IUI, consultations, semen analysis, sperm preparation, ultrasound scan and medicines. But this is not a definite list. You should ask for the details.

3. Is IUI cheaper than IVF?

Yes, usually IUI is more affordable than IVF, since it does not require egg retrieval, fertilization of eggs in a laboratory and embryo development. But the best method for you to choose depends on your fertility condition.

4. How many cycles of IUI are required?

It cannot be determined accurately since the success rate of conception varies from one couple to another. Some may become pregnant during their first IUI, others need additional attempts. It depends on your age, fertility problem, sperm quality, ovulation etc.

5. Is IUI Painful?

The IUI process is a rather easy one that is considered to cause no significant pain. However, some women may have cramping sensations. Should there be any serious symptoms such as severe pain, excessive bleeding, fever, etc., inform your fertility specialist.
