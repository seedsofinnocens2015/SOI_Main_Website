What is a Trigger Shot?

It is the hormone injection that is administered during the last days of ovarian stimulation during the IVF procedure. The main purpose of this procedure is to start the maturation of eggs for their further collection.

The injection replicates the natural increase of the LH that takes place during ovulation to stimulate the eggs to be prepared for fertilization.

Based on your choice of treatment, your fertility doctor may prescribe one of the following triggers to you:

  • Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG)
  • GnRH Agonist IVF Trigger injection
  • Both Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG) and GnRH Agonist

    • Based on your age, ovarian reserve, hormone levels, and chances of OHSS (Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome), there is a particular trigger injection suitable for you.

  • It prepares the eggs to be fertilized.
  • It ensures the collection of the eggs before their spontaneous ovulation.

    When Is the IVF Trigger Shot Taken?

    The fertility doctor will track your development via:

  • Ultrasounds
  • Blood tests for hormones
  • Measuring follicles

    • Once the follicles have matured, the Trigger Shot IVF is taken about 36 hours before egg harvesting.

    The injection could be:

  • Taken at the fertility center
  • Self-injected at home
  • Into the subcutaneous region
  • Into the intramuscular region, depending on the type of drug you are taking

    • The injection must be taken at the exact scheduled time since even a slight delay can impact the development of the eggs.

    Importance of Timing

    Timing is among the most critical factors influencing the success rate of IVF.

    After taking the trigger shot IUI:

  • Ovulation normally takes place within 36-40 hours.
  • The egg collection is done before the process of ovulation.
  • Mature eggs are harvested from the ovaries.
  • The eggs are then fertilized with the sperm inside an IVF laboratory.
  • Development of embryos, which are later transferred to the womb, takes place.

    • Failure to take or miss the trigger shot could lead to:

  • Premature ovulation
  • Immature eggs
  • Canceled egg harvesting
  • Low fertilization rates

    • That is why fertility centers stress adherence to the schedule given by the doctor.

    What Occurs after the Trigger Shot IVF?

    After the trigger shot IVF is administered, patients are advised to go about their daily lives as they prepare for egg harvesting.

    After 36 hours, the process of egg harvesting takes place using mild sedation.

    After the harvesting process:

  • Oocytes are harvested from the ovaries.
  • Fertilization occurs in the laboratory.
  • Embryos are cultured for 3-5 days.
  • Selection of the best embryo takes place.
  • Planning for the transfer of the embryos is made.

    • what is a trigger shot side effects?

    An Trigger Shot is normally safe, though there are minor side effects.

    They include:

  • Abdominal pain
  • Bloating
  • Tenderness of breasts
  • Redness at the injection site
  • Nausea
  • Fatigue
  • Mood swings

    • Risk of Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS)

    Another complication, which is relatively rare but serious, is Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS).

    OHSS is caused by an excessive reaction of the ovaries to the drugs.

    Women suffering from PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) have a higher risk of OHSS.

    Symptoms of OHSS:

  • Severe bloating
  • Fast weight gain
  • Swelling of ovaries
  • Abdominal pain
  • Trouble breathing
  • Less frequent urination
  • Nausea and vomiting

    • Patients who have higher chances of developing OHSS are carefully monitored, and different triggers are prescribed at Seeds of Innocens IVF Center.

    Can the Trigger Shot Influence the Result of a Pregnancy Test?

    Yes,The trigger injections usually include hCG – the hormone that can be detected in the pregnancy test.

    If you take a pregnancy test immediately after the shot, you will get a false-positive result since the hormone stays in your body.

    Your doctor may advise you to:

  • Wait 10-14 days after the trigger injection before taking a home pregnancy test.
  • Have a Beta hCG test done for confirmation.

    • Rate of Success with IVF Trigger Shots

    It is worth noting that the trigger shot itself will not decide whether a woman gets pregnant. Instead, the trigger shot helps ensure successful egg maturation, contributing to the overall IVF process.

    This process is influenced by various factors including:

  • Timing of the trigger shot
  • Age of the patient
  • Quality of the egg
  • Quality of the sperm
  • Response of ovaries
  • Embryo quality
  • Health of the uterus

    • With proper administration of the trigger shots, chances of obtaining mature eggs for fertilization increase.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. What is an IVF Trigger Shot?

    An IVF Trigger Shot is a hormone shot that aids in maturing the eggs before retrieval.

    2. When is the trigger shot administered during the IVF cycle?

    Usually, it is administered 36 hours before egg retrieval when the follicles reach the required size.

    3. Can there be side effects from the trigger shot?

    Yes. It may cause mild bloating, abdominal pain, breast soreness, and exhaustion. There is a chance of developing OHSS (Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome).

    4. Can I take a pregnancy test after the trigger shot?

    It is better not to do that because most trigger shots include hCG hormone and therefore the test will give a false positive result.

    5. Why is timing crucial in the trigger shot?

    It is necessary to take the trigger shot precisely at the prescribed time to make sure that the eggs mature well enough to harvest before ovulation happens naturally.
