Factors That Determine IVF Costs in Jammu

1. Diagnostic Procedures

Before beginning IVF, you might have blood tests, hormone tests, ultrasounds, sperm analysis, and various other diagnostic procedures conducted on you. The number of tests performed will influence the initial cost of treatment.

2. Medications

To stimulate your ovaries during an IVF cycle, certain medications will be administered to you depending upon your personal requirements. Since the amount and duration of time for which these medications might be administered will differ among individuals, so too will the cost of these medications.

3. Laboratory Techniques

There are embryology and various other laboratory techniques involved in IVF treatment. You may require extra lab techniques to be done as per clinical needs.

4. Embryo Freezing

If there are any surplus embryos left after the process of embryo transfer, then these embryos could be stored for further use in the future.

5. Number of IVF Cycles

It is not true that all IVF cycles lead to a successful pregnancy. Couples might need more than one IVF cycle, according to the specifics of each case. It means that couples need to take into account not just the price of the first cycle but also further treatment costs.
