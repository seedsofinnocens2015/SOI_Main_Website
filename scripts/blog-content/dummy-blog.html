What is IUI?

IUI is one of the most accessible fertility treatment methods available. Sperm cells that pass a special filtration process are introduced to the uterus during the ovulation period. This means that there is no need for the sperm to travel long distances to find the egg and fertilize it.

This procedure helps nature with its work, but doesn't require complicated equipment like it's in the case of IVF.

Your doctor will recommend you have cost of iui treatment Meerut if:

  • You have problems with conceiving and the cause is not clear
  • Your male partner has a problem with sperm cells (low number or poor mobility)
  • You have endometriosis or ovulation disorders
  • There are some problems with cervical mucus
  • You use donor sperm

IUI Process – A Step-by-Step Guide

IUI treatment is fast and almost painless:

Step 1: Consultation and testing: You consult with a doctor who will recommend several tests for both yourself and your partner.

Step 2: Egg development induction: A small amount of medication can induce the production of either one or two healthy eggs.

Step 3: Observing the process: Several ultrasound procedures will observe the development of your follicles so that the procedure can be done on the right day.

Step 4: Injection: This procedure will induce ovulation.

Step 5: Sperm preparation: Fresh sperm will be processed and prepared in the laboratory in about one hour.

Step 6: IUI procedure: A soft, flexible tube will introduce the prepared sperm directly to your womb, and it will take only 5-10 minutes without anesthesia.

Step 7: Two weeks waiting: Take a pregnancy test after two weeks.

IUI Success Rate: The Real Deal

The success rate per IUI cycle is about 10% to 20%, based on age, cause of infertility and sperm quality. Success rates are higher in women younger than 35 and go down with age.

Success rates build up, and many couples conceive after three to four cycles. If IUI fails a few times, your doctor will probably recommend you try IVF.

What increases your chances?

  • Starting treatment at an early stage
  • Maintaining a healthy weight
  • Not smoking or drinking alcohol
  • Managing stress by sleeping well, exercising and eating properly

Is IUI Painful?

IUI is a very safe procedure. You might experience some cramping similar to menstrual cramps, but it's brief. Some risks include infection and multiple pregnancies (twins) if more eggs are released. This is prevented with careful monitoring.
