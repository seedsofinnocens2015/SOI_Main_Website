What Is IUI Treatment?

In IUI treatment, which stands for Intrauterine Insemination, specially processed semen is placed directly into the womb of the woman during ovulation. This is done to enhance the chances of fertilization since the process will help the sperm cells to easily reach the egg cell. The process of IUI treatment is fast and painless.

Who Can Opt for IUI Treatment?

For patients who are suffering from the following conditions, the doctors will suggest the use of IUI procedure step by step:

  • Mild male infertility
  • Unexplained infertility
  • Cervical mucus problem
  • Ovulation problems
  • Mild endometriosis
  • Ejaculation problems
  • Single women or same-sex couples with donor sperm

    • When the fallopian tubes are blocked or when the patient suffers from severe male infertility or severe endometriosis, then IUI treatment is not suitable for such patients.

    IUI procedure step by step

    A step-by-step IUI procedure reduces the burden of treatment. What occurs during the IUI procedure?

    Step 1: Fertility Consultation

    The first step in the IUI procedure step by step is to consult with a fertility expert. During this step, the doctor will examine the following:

  • Medical history
  • Women's menstrual cycle
  • Past pregnancies
  • Life style
  • Fertility issues

    • Several tests may be conducted to see if the IUI process is right for you:

  • Blood hormone test
  • Ultrasound scan
  • Semen analysis
  • Ovulation monitoring
  • Fallopian tube checkup

    • Step 2: Ovulation Monitoring

    This is another step in the step-by-step IUI procedure process.

    Ovulation is monitored using the following methods:

  • Ultrasound scan
  • Blood hormone test
  • Ovulation kits

    • One of the most important aspects of the successful IUI treatment process is timing.

    Step 3: Fertility Medications (When Necessary)

    Some women have a natural cycle IUI, while other women use fertility medications to induce egg.

    Commonly used fertility medications are as follows:

  • Clomiphene Citrate
  • Letrozole
  • Injectable gonadotropins

    • The aim is to make one or more healthy follicles.

    Step 4: Trigger Shot

    When the follicles become sufficiently large, your doctor will give you an injection of Trigger Shot. The injection:

  • Matures the eggs
  • Initiates the ovulation process
  • Ensures accurate timing of the insemination process

    • The insemination procedure is scheduled for 24 to 36 hours following the trigger shot.

    Step 5: Sperm Collection and Preparation

    On the day of the procedure, the male partner provides a sperm sample; if using donor sperm, it is appropriately prepared.

    The sperm sample undergoes a procedure known as sperm washing in the laboratory. This entails:

  • Cleaning out the impurities
  • Getting rid of dead sperm
  • Concentrating active sperm

    • This procedure greatly increases the probability of conception through the IUI treatment procedure.

    Step 6: The IUI Procedure

    This step is the core of the IUI procedure, step by step.

    The procedure takes only 10-15 minutes.

    The steps involved in the process include the following:

  • Lying comfortably on the examination table.
  • Using a speculum for insertion into the vagina.
  • Passing a thin, flexible catheter through the cervix.
  • Inseminated sperm are put directly in the uterus.

    • The procedure is relatively painless, but some women may experience cramping.

    Anesthesia and hospitalization are not required.

    Step 7: Rest Post-Procedure

    Women can rest for 10-15 minutes after the IUI procedure.

    Women can:

  • Return to work
  • Perform daily tasks
  • Follow their routines

    • Progesterone pills are prescribed for the support of implantation.

    Step 8: The Two Weeks Wait

    The last phase of the step-by-step IUI procedure is one of the most emotional stages. During the two weeks after the procedure:

  • Implantation occurs.
  • Some spotting or cramping can be experienced.
  • Symptoms of pregnancy will occur.
  • Taking a pregnancy test can lead to false results.

    • Step 9: Pregnancy Test

    Approximately 14 days after your IUI procedure, your doctor will suggest that you undergo a blood pregnancy test.

  • Positive result
  • Care during pregnancy starts.
  • Ultrasound scan is scheduled.
  • Negative result
  • Next course of treatment is discussed by your doctor.

    • Further IUI procedures or IVF Treatment In India are suggested as per the condition of your infertility.

    Duration of IUI Process

    The full process of IUI usually takes about 2-4 weeks depending upon the menstrual cycle.

  • First appointment: 1 day
  • Monitoring ovulation: 10-14 days
  • Trigger injection: 1 day
  • IUI procedure: 10-15 minutes
  • Pregnancy test: After 14 days

    • Success Rate of IUI Treatment

    The success rate of the IUI treatment procedure depends on various factors like:

  • Age of the woman
  • Quality of the egg
  • Quality of sperm
  • Infertility cause
  • Cycles of treatments

    • On average, the pregnancy rate per cycle is 10%-20%, with a higher success rate among young women.

    However, if the IUI process does not prove to be successful after several cycles, fertility specialists will recommend that patients undergo IVF Treatment In India.

    • What are the risks involved in IUI?

    The IUI procedure, in general, is pretty safe, and some of the minimal risks associated with it are:

  • Cramping
  • Spotting
  • Infection (very rare)
  • Multiple pregnancies (in cases where fertility drugs are being taken)
  • Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (very rare in case of injectable drugs)

    • Your fertility specialist will keep a check on the cycle to avoid such complications.

    When Should IVF Be Opted For Instead?

    Though IUI treatment is highly beneficial for several couples, IVF can be opted for if any of the following occurs:

  • Both fallopian tubes are blocked.
  • There is severe male infertility.
  • Several IUI treatment sessions have been unsuccessful.
  • Age has become an infertility factor for women.
  • There is severe endometriosis.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. Is the IUI procedure painful?

    No, the IUI procedure is not usually painful. Women experience some mild discomfort or cramps during the insertion of the catheter inside the uterus. The procedure is fast and takes about 10-15 minutes to be completed without the need for anesthesia.

    2. How effective is IUI treatment?

    The effectiveness of IUI treatment relies on several parameters, such as a woman's age, the quality of sperm, ovulation, and the reasons for infertility. On average, the success rate is 10-20 percent per cycle; however, there are better chances for younger women (under 35 years old).

    3. How much time does the IUI process take?

    The whole IUI process can take 2-4 weeks, including evaluation of fertility, ovulation tracking, medication intake (if necessary), insemination, and a two-week wait to get pregnant test results.

    4. Who is an ideal candidate for IUI treatment?

    IUI treatment can be prescribed to couples with infertility of unknown reasons, mild male infertility, ovulation problems, abnormal cervical mucus, and mild endometriosis. IUI is also a good choice for single women or gay couples with the help of donor sperm.

    5. What is the number of cycles that I have to undergo for IUI before opting for IVF?

    The general recommendation by fertility experts is that one needs to undergo 3 to 6 IUI cycles before IVF treatment in India. This will depend on a range of factors, such as your age and your diagnosis of infertility.
