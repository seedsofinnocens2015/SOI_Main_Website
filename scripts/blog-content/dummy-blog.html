What Do Fertility Doctors in Srinagar Treat?

Fertility doctors in Srinagar treat different causes of infertility in women and men.

These can be:

  • Problems with Ovulation: If ovulation is irregular or absent, you may face problems conceiving. Conditions like PCOS might interfere with ovulation.
  • Tubal Issues: Damage to the fallopian tube will prevent the egg and sperm from meeting.
  • Endometriosis: Some cases of endometriosis have been seen to be related to infertility and may require different kinds of treatment.
  • Male Infertility: Low sperm count or issues with the movement of sperm can cause infertility.
  • Reduced Ovarian Reserve: With less availability of eggs, it will become difficult to conceive.
  • Unexplained Infertility: There might be some cases where no apparent reason can be found for infertility through investigations.

What Infertility Treatments Can Be Provided in Srinagar?

An experienced infertility specialist in Srinagar will never suggest going for IVF treatment immediately. The treatment plan depends on the causes of infertility, age, and other medical issues.

IUI

Intrauterine insemination is a procedure that involves placing prepared sperm directly into the uterus close to ovulation. It can be suggested for some fertility issues.

IVF

In vitro fertilisation is a procedure that includes stimulating the ovaries, retrieving the eggs, fertilising them with sperm in the laboratory, and transferring the embryo into the uterus.

ICSI

Intracytoplasmic sperm injection involves injecting one sperm into the egg during the process of fertilisation. It can be suggested in some cases, especially when there is male infertility or previous fertilisation difficulties.

Fertility Preservation

Some patients can consider egg or sperm freezing depending on the individual case and future fertility. The main thing is that the procedure should be selected according to your needs, not just because it is the most advanced one.
