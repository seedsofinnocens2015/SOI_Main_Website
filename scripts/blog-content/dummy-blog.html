What is Implantation Bleeding?

Implantation bleeding is normally the earliest indication that you are pregnant, and it happens when a fertilized egg implants itself into the wall of the uterus. How does implantation bleeding occur? During the process of embedding the embryo into the wall of the uterus, the rupture of minor blood vessels may result in bleeding.
