    • Understanding Infertility

    Infertility is usually defined as not being able to conceive after:

  • 12 months of regular unprotected intercourse (for women under 35)
  • 6 months of regular unprotected intercourse (for women above 35)

    • Also, infertility can affect both partners, so it is not only a “woman's problem”, even if tests sometimes focus there first.

    Female infertility causes may include:

  • PCOS
  • Endometriosis
  • Blocked fallopian tubes
  • Poor ovarian reserve
  • Ovulation disorders
  • Uterine abnormalities
  • Age-related fertility decline

    • Male infertility causes may include:

  • Low sperm count
  • Poor sperm motility
  • Abnormal sperm morphology
  • Varicocele
  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Genetic disorders

    • A reliable fertility clinic in Muzaffarpur checks both partners before planning any treatment, because guessing usually wastes time.

    • ICSI Treatment

    ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection) is a strong choice when male infertility is the main issue. In this method, one healthy sperm is injected directly into the egg, which helps fertilization happen more reliably.

    IUI Treatment

    Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) is often advised for:

  • Mild male infertility
  • Unexplained infertility
  • Ovulation disorders

    • Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET)

    Frozen embryos can be transferred in a later cycle. This gives more flexibility and can support better timing for implantation.

    Fertility Preservation

    This can be considered for:

  • Cancer patients
  • Women delaying pregnancy
  • Low ovarian reserve
  • Social egg freezing

    • Donor Egg & Donor Sperm Programs

    These programs may suit people with:

  • Premature ovarian failure
  • Poor egg quality
  • Severe male infertility
