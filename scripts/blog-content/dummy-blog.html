What Is the IVF Success Rate in India?

The success rate for IVF in India typically lies between 40% to 70% per cycle of IVF depending upon the fertility status and treatment methods followed by the couple.

There are various factors that affect the success rate of IVF in India such as:

  • Age of the female partner
  • Quality of eggs and ovarian reserve
  • Quality of sperm
  • Quality of embryos
  • Health of the uterus
  • Lifestyle

    • Experience of the fertility team

    IVF Success Rate in India by Age

    One of the most important determinants of IVF success rate in India by age is the poor quality of eggs due to aging.

    Women Under 35 Years

    Women under the age of 35 usually exhibit the highest IVF success rate in India since they usually have good quality eggs, embryos, and ovarian reserve. Most couples from this age category usually conceive during their first or second IVF cycle.

    Women Between 35 and 37 Years

    For women between 35 and 37 years old, the success rate of the treatment is quite high in India. Although fertility slowly starts to diminish, the personalized treatment procedure, along with the advancement of embryology, continues to produce good results.

    Women Between 38 and 40 Years

    The IVF success rate of women between 38 and 40 years old starts to decrease due to low quality of eggs and chromosomal disorders. Nevertheless, it is still possible for most of them to conceive successfully using fertility treatments.

    Women Above 40 Years

    Even though fertility starts to diminish after 40 years, it is still possible for some women to conceive. Fertility experts might recommend additional procedures such as the use of donor eggs and genetic embryo screening, depending on the case.

    IVF Treatment Success Factors

    There is no one-size-fits-all solution to fertility issues as each couple faces different challenges. Knowing those factors helps raise the effectiveness of IVF in India.

    1. Female Age

    Female age impacts egg number and quality. Early consultation with an IVF Specialist in India tends to lead to positive treatment results.

    2. Egg Quality and Ovarian Reserve

    AMH and AFC (Antral Follicle Count) tests help evaluate ovarian reserve. Better ovarian reserve increases the chances of IVF success.

    3. Sperm Quality

    Around half of infertility causes are linked to males. Good sperm count, motility, morphology, and DNA integrity greatly increase the chance of fertilization.

    In cases where there are problems with sperm, ICSI technique may be applied.

    4. Embryo Quality

    Better-developed embryos have greater chances of being implanted. Modern embryology labs use sophisticated systems of embryo grading to raise the probability of successful treatment with IVF in India.

    5. Uterine Health

    Uterus is crucial for successful IVF treatment. Uterine conditions like fibroids, polyps, thin endometrium, or other abnormalities decrease the success rate of IVF if not treated.

    6. Lifestyle and General Well-being

    There are certain simple things that one can do to improve their fertility results. The list includes:

  • Keeping normal body weight
  • A proper diet
  • Physical exercises
  • No smoking and drinking
  • Reducing stress
  • Sufficient amount of sleep

    • A healthy lifestyle will help you achieve good reproductive results, increase your chances for a successful IVF procedure in India.
