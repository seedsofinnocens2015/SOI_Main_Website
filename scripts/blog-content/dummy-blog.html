What is In Vitro Fertilization?

In vitro fertilization (IVF) is an assisted reproductive technology where eggs and sperms are combined in a lab to create an embryo. The embryo is then implanted into the womb of the woman to conceive a baby. In cases of infertility issues like blocked fallopian tubes, male infertility issues, lack of ovulation, low ovarian reserve, unexplained infertility, and failure of other fertility treatments, this technique is advised. This process is different for everyone. However, the first step involves the evaluation of your medical background and fertility tests.

Influential Factors of the Cost of IVF Treatment in Delhi

It is impossible to mention a specific amount that will be charged to each couple. However, the following factors might influence the cost of the IVF procedure in Delhi.

1. Fertility Drugs

The use of hormonal medication and injections is quite widespread in cases of ovarian stimulation. In turn, the dosage and the period of treatment will differ from patient to patient. This point will influence the overall cost of the treatment.

2. Type of IVF Procedure

There is a possibility that the standard IVF procedure may differ in cost from the procedure with ICSI. The latter involves injecting a sperm into an egg and is used in cases of male factor infertility only.

3. Freezing of the Embryos

In case the embryos need to be frozen for further transfer, additional charges should be paid. Meanwhile, the transfer of frozen embryos will also have its own cost.

4. Genetic Testing

In some cases, couples will need to undergo genetic testing of the embryos according to their medical history and conditions. This procedure will increase the total cost of the treatment.

5. No. of IVF Cycles

While some may conceive after one cycle, others might require further treatment sessions. But the no. of cycles cannot be determined beforehand since the success rate of IVF depends upon individual cases.

6. Donor Eggs or Sperm

In case of medically suggested use of donor eggs or sperm, the process and costs would differ from traditional IVF.
