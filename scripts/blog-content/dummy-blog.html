What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI (Intrauterine Insemination) is one of the least complicated fertility treatment techniques. During the procedure, the gynecologist selects the best sperm sample and introduces it into the uterus through a thin tube. This method gives an opportunity to reduce the distance that sperm have to travel to meet the egg.

The whole procedure takes about 5 to 10 minutes. Moreover, IUI procedure is almost painless, non-surgical, and does not require any admission to the hospital.

Who May Be Suitable for IUI?

IUI could be an option if:

  • You have been trying to conceive for one year or more without success
  • You have mild male fertility issues such as low sperm count or poor motility
  • You have unexplained infertility
  • You have problems with cervical mucus
  • You have irregular ovulation, such as in PCOS
  • You are a single woman or a same-sex couple using donor sperm
  • For IUI to be successful, you should have at least one healthy and open fallopian tube.
