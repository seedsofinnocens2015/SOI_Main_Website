An IVF doctor in Delhi NCR is a fertility specialist that works on diagnosing and treating fertility problems that can make conception difficult. Depending on the health of the patient, the doctor will suggest various fertility treatments including ovulation induction, IUI and IVF.

What Transpires During an IVF Consultation?

An initial consultation with a fertility specialist for IVF in Delhi NCR usually starts with an exploration of your medical and fertility background.

The doctor may want to know about your:

  • Menstrual cycle
  • Prior pregnancies
  • Past infertility treatment
  • Other medical problems
  • Past surgeries
  • Drugs
  • Family background
  • Partner's fertility history

According to your case, you may be advised to undergo hormonal tests, ultrasound scans, ovarian reserve tests or semen analysis.

Upon analyzing your reports, the doctor will be able to offer various treatment options.

Is IVF a Suitable Infertility Treatment for Everyone in Delhi NCR?

Not necessarily; it doesn't mean that IVF is the ideal treatment option for everyone.

Some people may conceive with simple changes in their lifestyle, ovulation therapy, IUI treatment or by curing the root cause of their infertility.

Others may require IVF due to their blocked fallopian tubes, male factor infertility, low ovarian reserve or failure of other treatments.

Choosing the right infertility treatment in Delhi should thus start with proper fertility evaluation.
