Which Factors Influence IUI Costs in Lucknow?

There are certain factors that may affect IUI costs in Lucknow.

1. Fertility Tests and Examination

A doctor may go through the medical history of the couple before suggesting IUI and recommend tests, which could be anything from semen analysis, ovulation tests, hormone tests, and uterus and fallopian tube examination. The number of such tests could increase or decrease the overall cost.

2. Fertility Drugs

In some cases, women may need fertility drugs to aid in ovulation. The type and quantity of drug will depend on each individual's treatment schedule. Not all women require the same drug.

3. Ultrasound Scans

Ultrasound may be conducted to check follicular growth and determine the ideal time for insemination. The number of ultrasounds will differ from person to person.

4. Sperm Preparation

For IUI, a semen sample is prepared in a laboratory to be ready for insemination. The fees for laboratory work may be included in the package or charged separately.

5. Other Medical Needs

In some cases, there is a need for extra tests and consultation from specialists prior to the treatment. Such additional needs can affect the total cost of treatment and treatment itself.

What Does IUI Include?

IUI procedure is most often an outpatient one; it doesn't require surgery or anaesthetics. Steps involved in IUI are as follows:

Step 1: Initial Consultation

The fertility specialist analyses the medical history and decides on the necessary investigation.

Step 2: Ovulation Determination

The doctor examines the menstrual cycle and uses ultrasound scanning or medication to find out the appropriate time for insemination.

Step 3: Sperm preparation

The semen sample is prepared in the laboratory.

Step 4: Insemination

The processed sperm cells are inserted into the womb using a catheter. This process is relatively quick and straightforward, but a few people can experience discomfort or cramping.

Step 5: Follow-Up Care

The doctor will provide instructions on when the pregnancy test should be done and if there is any need for follow-up care. It all depends on how and when the procedure is done, and on the individual patient's medical needs.

When Is IUI a Possible Solution?

In some cases, IUI may be a good solution for a couple that has been evaluated for their fertility. Depending on the case at hand, it can be considered for:

  • Couples with certain types of unexplained infertility
  • Individuals suffering from difficulties with ovulation, and who can be treated for them
  • Specific cases of mild male factor infertility
  • People using donor sperm, where this is legal and possible to do

IUI cannot be used when both fallopian tubes are obstructed or where other fertility challenges are very serious. In those cases, there is another form of treatment that may suit.

IUI vs IVF: Know the Difference in Price

Patients who are researching fertility treatments compare IUI and IVF. But these procedures are different and are suggested for various medical conditions.

In IUI, the prepared sperm is placed into the uterus during ovulation. In IVF, the eggs are collected, fertilised with sperm in the laboratory, and the embryo is transferred.
