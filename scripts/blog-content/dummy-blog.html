What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI treatment is a procedure wherein sperm cells are injected directly into the uterus at the time of ovulation when the egg is released.

In other words, IUI helps the sperm reach the egg and increase the chances of conception within the body naturally.

This process normally includes three steps - monitoring of ovulation, processing of the sperm sample in the lab, and injection of the processed sperm into the uterus using a thin flexible tube.

IUI normally lasts only a few minutes and does not need anaesthesia. However, depending on your condition, your doctor might suggest some fertility medications.

Factors Affecting the Cost of IUI Treatment in Kolkata

There are some factors that affect the cost of IUI treatment in Kolkata.

1. Fertility tests: These include semen analysis, hormonal tests, ultrasound scanning, and checking if the fallopian tubes are open.

2. Fertility medicines: There are some cases when some women will need medications for ovulation. The kind and dosage of such medications will have an effect on the total cost of treatment.

3. Number of cycles: In IUI treatment, the procedure is done per menstrual cycle. There are some who conceive during their first try, while there are some who need to go through more cycles.

4. Medical condition: There are various causes of infertility, age, sperm quality, and response to treatment, among others.

5. Clinic facilities: There are consultation charges, laboratory testing, monitoring, and package contents.

Before treatment begins, ensure that you have been quoted an estimate for a full cycle and talk about what will happen in case you don’t conceive.

Who Should Go for IUI Treatment?

The following is a list of some cases where IUI is recommended:

  • Couples with mild male-factor infertility based on the quality of sperm.
  • Cases where there are ovulation problems and they can be treated.
  • Infertility of unknown cause after proper assessment.
  • Situations with donor sperm when it is legally and medically required.

But, it doesn’t mean that IUI is for all couples facing fertility issues. In situations where the tubes are blocked, the cases of severe sperm abnormalities, and other fertility problems, the couples may consider other treatment methods.

A specialist in fertility can examine both partners and determine the treatment method based on their age and medical history and the outcome of tests conducted.

5. Is IUI painful?

There is minimal discomfort, though minor cramping or light bleeding may occur. Consult your physician if you have severe pain, heavy bleeding, or fever.
