What Treatments Can Infertility Problems Be Cured With?

It depends on the cause of infertility. An infertility expert in Haldwani will be able to offer you different kinds of treatment depending on your case.

Ovulation Treatment

Drugs can be prescribed for the treatment of ovulation in case of infertility due to the irregularity or absence of ovulation.

IUI Treatment

The IUI treatment involves the insertion of the prepared sperm into the woman’s uterus near the time of ovulation. The treatment is suggested based on the evaluation of the couple’s fertility.

IVF Treatment

The in vitro fertilization treatment, or IVF, involves the process of obtaining the eggs, their fertilization by the sperm cells in the laboratory, and then placing a healthy embryo into the uterus. The IVF treatment is recommended in case of blocked fallopian tubes, male infertility, low ovarian reserve, endometriosis, and unexplained infertility.

ICSI Treatment

The intracytoplasmic sperm injection, or ICSI, is a technique where one sperm is inserted into the egg during fertilization in the laboratory. The ICSI treatment can be suggested for some cases of male infertility or previous difficulties with fertilization.

An infertility specialist will be able to decide if you need ICSI treatment or not.
