What Is IVF?

In vitro Fertilisation (IVF) is a method of assisted reproductive technology in which the egg and sperm are fertilized outside of the body in a lab. After the fertilization process, the embryo is observed in the lab and then transferred into the uterus during the process.

IVF is an option for couples who suffer from such problems as blocked fallopian tubes, ovulation problems, endometriosis, male factor infertility, unexplained infertility, or age-related infertility.

These include ovarian stimulation, monitoring, egg retrieval, fertilization, embryo growth, and embryo transfer.

Factors Influencing IVF Procedure Cost in Ghaziabad

Several factors could influence the cost of your IVF procedure in Ghaziabad. Knowing what these factors are will assist you in planning financially.

1. Infertility Diagnosis

Infertility diagnosis plays a major role in determining your treatment plan. In the case where a woman is facing difficulties with ovulation, she will be treated differently from a woman suffering from tubal and male factor infertility.

2. Infertility Medication

In most cases, IVF requires the use of medications to stimulate ovaries. The type, amount, and period of time during which you receive medications will depend on your body's ovarian reserve and response to ovarian stimulation. This means that different people will pay different fees for their medication.

3. Tests and Investigations

Before undergoing an IVF procedure, there are several tests that you might need to take. The tests include blood tests, hormone tests, semen analysis, ovulation assessment, ovarian reserve test, and other tests.

4. Laboratory Services

An IVF procedure includes a wide range of laboratory services. Depending on your treatment plan, additional laboratory procedures might be recommended.

5. Embryo Freezing and Storage

There are cases when couples decide to or are recommended to freeze embryos to use them in the future. Embryo freezing and storage can come with extra costs depending on the fertility clinic and what procedures are needed.

6. Treatment Cycles

The number of treatment cycles that would be needed in the course of IVF may influence the total cost of the treatment as well. The result of IVF treatment varies among people, and there are cases when additional treatment is needed according to the situation.
