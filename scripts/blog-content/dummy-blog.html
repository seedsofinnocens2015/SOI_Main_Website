Factors Affecting IVF Treatment Costs in Agra

Costs associated with IVF treatment in Agra vary widely since fertility treatment is personal. There are various factors that can affect the costs involved.

1. Fertility Tests

Before commencing IVF treatment, there may be a need for testing to know the condition of the patient's eggs, hormones, sperm, uterus, among others. Different tests may be required by different couples.

2. Medications

During the IVF process, medication will be required by some people to stimulate the ovaries as well as the growth of the follicles. Different patients will have different requirements for medication.

3. Number of IVF Cycles

Some couples conceive after undergoing just one IVF cycle while others may have to do it several times. This means the total cost will increase with the number of cycles.

4. Other Fertility Treatments

Other treatments or lab procedures might be required based on the patient’s health condition. They may add to the total treatment cost.

5. Embryo Freezing and Storage

If there are any suitable embryos left after one cycle of the IVF treatment, then embryo freezing might be considered. The storage fee might be separate from the basic IVF package.

What is the IUI Cost in Agra?

IUI, also known as Intrauterine Insemination, is another form of assisted reproductive technology used in some fertility disorders.

IUI costs in Agra are usually lower than IVF costs. Depending on the medicines, tests, monitoring, and clinic, the cost might vary between about ₹5,000 to ₹20,000 per cycle.

But IUI might not suit every couple as well. Factors like age, ovarian reserves, sperm parameters, status of fallopian tubes, and past fertility history need to be assessed by a fertility specialist to decide between IUI and IVF.

Comparison of IVF and IUI and the Recommendation of Which Treatment Should Be Considered

IVF and IUI are two different fertility treatment options that depend on medical conditions.

With IUI treatment, the fertilized egg is introduced into the uterus. However, in the case of IVF treatment, an egg is harvested, fertilized with sperm, and the embryo created is implanted in the uterus.

The medical practitioner considers certain factors such as:

  • Age of the woman
  • Length of infertility
  • Ovarian reserve
  • Sperm quality
  • Condition of fallopian tubes
  • Previously tried fertility treatment
  • Medical background

Thus, it is advisable not to consider cost as the sole criterion when choosing one treatment over another.
