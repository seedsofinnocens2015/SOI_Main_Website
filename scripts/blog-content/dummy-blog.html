Are you facing Infertility?

Infertility is a problem faced by more people than you think. According to several studies done across the globe, around one in six couples finds it difficult to conceive without any assistance. Infertility may affect both the male and female partner with equal chances; hence, a complete fertility test for both partners becomes imperative.

Common factors that cause infertility include:

  • Irregular or lack of ovulation
  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)
  • Endometriosis
  • Fallopian tube blockage or damage
  • Poor ovarian reserve
  • A decrease in fertility due to age
  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Low sperm count or poor sperm movement
  • Obesity, smoking, alcohol abuse, and chronic stress due to lifestyle issues

With an early diagnosis, fertility experts at Seeds of Innocens IVF India will be able to advise the best possible fertility treatment in India or even other advanced treatment options.

Why is IVF one of the most effective fertility treatments?

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) has been known to provide outstanding success rates to couples with challenging fertility problems.

Unlike the natural process, with IVF it becomes possible to closely monitor fertilization and embryo development under controlled laboratory conditions. The procedure helps improve the chances of transferring healthy embryos to achieve a successful pregnancy.

  • Fertility preservation before medical interventions

These days, IVF should not be viewed as a treatment of last resort – it is an efficient scientific method used by numerous couples.

IVF Treatment at Seeds of Innocens IVF

An understanding of the various stages of IVF treatment in India will not only help you prepare better but also reduce stress.

Stage I: Fertility Assessment

The first stage in your IVF treatment process in India involves a consultation with one of our fertility specialists as well as a thorough assessment of your infertility.

We assess:

  • Hormonal levels
  • Ovarian reserve
  • Ultrasound results
  • Uterine well-being
  • Semen analysis
  • Pregnancy history
  • Medical and lifestyle conditions

Using the above results, a customized treatment program is formulated.

Step 2: Ovarian Stimulation

Fertility drugs are administered to stimulate the ovaries and produce multiple mature eggs within a single menstrual cycle.

At this step, frequent ultrasounds and blood tests measure follicle development and hormone levels so that medication dosages can be modified.

Step 3: Egg Collection

Upon the maturation of the eggs, egg retrieval is performed through a non-surgical procedure using light sedation.

This process generally takes about 20-30 minutes, and patients can go back home after a couple of hours.

Step 4: Fertilization and Embryo Cultivation

The retrieved eggs are fertilized by sperm in our state-of-the-art embryology laboratory. According to the couple's condition, additional methods can be suggested, such as:

  • ICSI
  • Blastocyst culture
  • PGT
  • Embryo time-lapse imaging

Each embryo is evaluated to choose the best one for embryo transfer.

Step 5: Embryo Transfer

The chosen embryo is transferred into the uterus through the use of a very thin catheter.

The procedure is very simple, painless, and does not involve any surgical intervention.

After the procedure, you will be provided with medication and lifestyle advice before the pregnancy test.
