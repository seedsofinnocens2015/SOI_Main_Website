What Is IVF Treatment?

IVF or in vitro fertilisation is a type of fertility treatment whereby eggs are removed from the ovaries of the female and fertilized using sperm in a laboratory.

Usually, an IVF procedure consists of several steps, such as ovarian stimulation, monitoring, egg retrieval, fertilisation, embryo development, and embryo transfer. Variables That May Affect the Cost of IVF in Lucknow

Some of the variables that might influence the ivf treatment cost in lucknow include:

1. Fertility Investigations

Various investigations may need to be carried out before beginning the procedure. These might include blood tests, ultrasound scanning, semen analysis, and any other relevant investigation. The type and quantity of investigation could impact the overall cost of the treatment.

2. Fertility Drugs

In most cases, fertility treatment will involve hormonal drugs used to stimulate the production of eggs by the ovaries. The dose and period for which one uses these medications will depend on the patient's requirement and could thus influence the overall cost.

3. Lab Work Involved in the IVF Procedure

One of the integral parts of fertility treatment is the work done in the lab. Depending on the requirement of the couple, either conventional IVF or ICSI treatment would be suggested. ICSI is the procedure where a single sperm is injected directly into an egg.

4. Other Fertility Procedures

Some patients may require additional procedures according to their medical history and reaction to treatment. Some of those may include ICSI, assisted hatching, donor programs, or any other form of fertility treatment.

Hence, it is essential to consider the total cost of IVF in Lucknow while considering the overall procedure rather than just the initial.
