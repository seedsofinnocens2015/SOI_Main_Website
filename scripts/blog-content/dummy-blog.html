What Should You Look for in the Best IVF Doctor in Ranchi?

If you look for the best IVF doctors in Ranchi on the internet, you will find many names. But rather than deciding on the spot, compare the following key criteria.

1. Relevant Fertility Experience

The doctor must have proper qualifications and experience in reproductive medicine and infertility treatment. It is even better if he has handled cases that are similar to yours. For instance, the treatment of PCOS can differ widely from the treatment of blocked fallopian tubes, low ovarian reserve or male infertility.

2. Individualized Treatment Plan

Each case of infertility is unique.

The best IVF specialist in Ranchi should first evaluate your medical background and order the relevant tests before recommending IVF.

Ask the doctor:

  • What could be the reason for infertility?
  • What tests are needed?
  • Does testing of both partners require?
  • Is IVF really necessary in our case?
  • What other treatment options do we have?
  • What are the chances of success in our case?

This will help you understand why certain treatment is recommended.

3. Clear Communication

Infertility treatment entails various steps, drugs and visits, and your physician should inform you on the same in simple language, and not in complex medical terms. Do not hesitate to ask any questions on treatment, associated risks, expected outcomes and prices.

You can see a fertility expert in Ranchi in case:

What Fertility Treatments Are Offered in Ranchi?

Fertility clinics in Ranchi offer different fertility treatments depending on the needs of the patient. The current listings reveal such procedures as IVF, IUI, ICSI and fertility tests offered by several Ranchi fertility centres.

IUI

Intrauterine insemination is a procedure when prepared sperm is placed into the uterus close to ovulation. This procedure can be recommended for some patients with fertility problems.

IVF

In vitro fertilization is a procedure when ovaries are stimulated, eggs are retrieved, fertilized in the lab and an embryo is transferred to the uterus.

ICSI

ICSI means intracytoplasmic sperm injection, where a single sperm is injected into an egg. This may be an option in case of male-factor infertility or any prior fertilization issue.

Embryo Freezing

The eligible embryos can be frozen for later use depending upon the treatment plan.

Fertility Preservation

This procedure can be opted by individuals who want to preserve their fertility for future use.

A fertility specialist in Ranchi can assist you in choosing the right one based on your medical history.
