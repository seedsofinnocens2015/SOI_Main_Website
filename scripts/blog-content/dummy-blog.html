What Does an IVF Doctor Do in Jammu?

An IVF doctor is a fertility specialist who diagnoses and treats any problem that might be hindering the natural process of conception. Depending on the situation, the doctor might recommend medications, ovulation induction, IUI or IVF.

A fertility specialist in Jammu will also assess conditions like:

  • Polycystic ovaries and ovulation disorders
  • Low ovarian reserve
  • Blocked or damaged fallopian tubes
  • Endometriosis
  • Recurrent pregnancy loss
  • Male factor infertility
  • Low sperm count or poor motility
  • Unexplained infertility
  • Fertility problems associated with age

The first step is generally a thorough consultation followed by proper fertility tests. Your treatment should be recommended according to your diagnosis and not in a general way.

What Types of Fertility Treatments Are Available in Jammu?

It all depends on the reason for infertility.

IUI

Intrauterine insemination entails the insertion of prepared sperm into the uterus at the time of ovulation. It can be recommended in some patients.

IVF

In vitro fertilization entails stimulating the ovaries, removing eggs, and fertilizing them in a laboratory before implanting an embryo in the uterus.

ICSI

It entails the direct insertion of a sperm into an egg. This might be recommended in male infertility cases among others.

Fertility Preservation

Egg and sperm freezing might be recommended when a person wishes to preserve his/her fertility for the future.

Not everyone needs all these procedures. A competent infertility doctor in Jammu would recommend what suits your medical condition.
