What is Covered in IVF Treatment?

While talking about IVF treatment cost in Gurgaon, it is essential to know what all is covered in the quoted package.

Usually, there are many processes involved in an IVF cycle and some of them are:

1. Fertility evaluation:

This involves review of medical history by the doctor along with some tests such as hormone tests, ultrasound, semen test, ovarian reserve tests and other investigations as per the need.

2. Egg retrieval:

At the right stage, eggs are removed from the ovaries using a process known as oocyte retrieval.

3. Fertilisation and embryo development:

The eggs will be fertilised with the sperm in the laboratory and the development of the embryos will be followed.

4. Embryo transfer:

An embryo will be transferred to the uterus. Other embryos may also be cryopreserved for future use depending on the protocol of the treatment being done. Because of these reasons, the final IVF cost in Gurgaon may vary from patient to patient.

What Factors Determine the IVF Cost in Gurgaon?

There are various factors that may determine the total cost of IVF in Gurgaon.

1. Age and ovarian reserve

Age and ovarian reserve can affect the IVF treatment protocol including the need for drugs and how many cycles will be done.

2. Fertility diagnosis

Some conditions like diminished ovarian reserve, endometriosis, male infertility or even failed IVF cycles in the past may call for a customized treatment protocol.

3. Medications during IVF procedure

Medicines required for fertility can constitute a major chunk of the total treatment cost. The quantity and types of medicine used differ from one patient to another.

4. ICSI procedure

It is an advanced technique of fertilization in which a single sperm is injected into an egg. In case it is suggested, then it will increase the total cost of the treatment.

5. Freezing of embryos

Freezing of suitable embryos for further use can incur additional laboratory costs.

6. Genetic testing

For selected patients, it can be suggested to undergo genetic testing of embryos. Since it is an additional laboratory process, it increases the total cost of the treatment.

7. Number of IVF treatments

Depending upon whether the couple is successful in achieving pregnancy in the first attempt or requires more tries, the total treatment cost is higher than the cost of a single IVF procedure.

Frequently asked questions

1. What is the cost of IVF in Gurgaon?

Cost of IVF in Gurgaon depends on the program chosen, cost of drugs, tests, laboratory work, and other factors. The price of a standard IVF program is likely to be around ₹1 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh, however the precise estimate can be obtained at the clinic.

2. Does IVF package include medicines?

It depends. There could be some IVF packages that could include some medicines or investigations, whereas others might charge for them individually. Always ask for an itemized estimate.

3. Is ICSI part of the IVF procedure cost?

ICSI is not included in all IVF packages and may be done individually. In case ICSI procedure is recommended, always enquire if it is a part of the package or not.

4. What is the cost of a test tube baby in India?

Test tube baby cost essentially includes the cost of IVF treatment. The cost of a test tube baby varies according to the fertility clinic, the location, medications and procedures involved.

5. Is the IVF cost different for each individual?

Yes, Cost of IVF in Gurgaon varies because fertility treatment is tailor-made. Age, ovarian reserve, infertility diagnosis, medications, laboratory work, and prior treatments could make up the cost.
