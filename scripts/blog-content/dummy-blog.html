What Does an IVF Doctor Do in Agra?

An IVF doctor is a fertility expert who identifies and treats conditions that might prevent conception. IVF is not always the first choice of treatment for every couple. A competent fertility doctor will first determine the cause of infertility and then suggest the best treatment method.

When Should You Consult an IVF Specialist?

Many couples struggle with reproductive challenges, often leaving them uncertain about the right time to seek expert medical guidance.

Generally, scheduling a fertility assessment is recommended if natural conception has not occurred after twelve months of regular attempts. Because reproductive potential diminishes over time, women over 35 should consider seeking medical advice after six months.

You should also think about consulting an infertility doctor in Agra earlier if you experience any of the following factors:

  • Irregular, painful, or absent menstrual cycles
  • Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) or related endocrine disorders
  • Diagnosed endometriosis
  • Blockages or damage in the fallopian tubes
  • Diminished egg supply or low ovarian reserve
  • History of recurrent pregnancy loss
  • Unsuccessful outcome from previous fertility therapies
  • Abnormalities in sperm concentration, shape, or motility
  • Prior surgical procedures involving pelvic organs
  • Previous unsuccessful IUI or IVF attempts

It is important to remember that infertility can involve male factors, female factors, or a combination of both. Consequently, a comprehensive evaluation must address both partners equally.
