What is Unexplained Infertility?

Unexplained infertility is a condition in which a couple is unable to conceive a child, and the test results for the couple are normal. Couples who face this condition often feel confused and frustrated about the condition.

So, What is Unexplained Infertility? Unexplained infertility is a condition in which the couple experiences normal ovulation and open fallopian tubes with an adequate sperm count, yet they are unable to conceive a child. This is a complex condition that needs special attention.

Reasons for Unexplained Infertility

The reasons for unexplained infertility can be difficult to understand, and it can be a bit intricate since standard investigations do not reveal the reasons behind it. Some of the possible underlying causes of unexplained infertility include:

  • The quality of eggs, which is not revealed during standard investigations
  • Sperm dysfunction, which is not revealed during standard investigations
  • Failure of implantation
  • Hormonal imbalance at a micro level
  • Immune system problems

The underlying causes of unexplained infertility can be diagnosed using sophisticated investigations, and it requires expertise from an experienced ivf specialist.
