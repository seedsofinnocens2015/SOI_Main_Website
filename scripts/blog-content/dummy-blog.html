What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI (Intrauterine Insemination) is a short and simple procedure. Healthy sperm are then washed, concentrated, and placed directly into the womb around the time of ovulation. Also, it does not require surgery or any long stay in the hospital. Doctors may suggest IUI when:

  • There are cervical mucus problems
  • Sperm count or movement is mildly low
  • Ovulation is irregular with medicines
  • The cause of infertility is unexplained.

What Is IVF Treatment?

IVF (In Vitro Fertilization) is an advanced treatment. Eggs are retrieved from ovaries and fertilized in a lab using sperms. Embryos are then transferred to the uterus.

IVF is recommended when:

  • Fallopian Tubes are Blocked/Damaged
  • IUI has not been successful despite multiple attempts
  • Low Sperm Count
  • Endometriosis/ Reduced Egg Reserve
  • Woman is older than 35

What Is Covered In IVF Cost In Gorakhpur?

IVF cost in Gorakhpur generally includes the following aspects:

  • Consultation and initial tests
  • Medicines and injections for ovarian stimulation
  • Procedure for egg collection
  • Lab fertilization and embryo culture
  • Embryo transfer

Add-on procedures such as ICSI, embryo freezing, or donor services could incur additional costs. Make sure to receive an estimate in writing, detailing what is and is not included in the package.

Why Does The Cost Differ From One Patient To Another?

No two cases of infertility are alike. The ivf charges in gorakhpur or IUI cost will vary due to:

  • Woman's age and egg reserve
  • Type of infertility treatment required (IUI, IVF, or ICSI)
  • Dosage of medications, which varies between patients
  • Number of IVF cycles required
  • Additional tests or procedures suggested by your doctor

That is why the consultation with a specialist is more important than any list of prices available online.
