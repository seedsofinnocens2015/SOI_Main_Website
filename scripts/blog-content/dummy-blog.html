What Is IUI Treatment?

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) is one of the procedures used to help people conceive. The process includes insertion of specially prepared sperm into the uterus of a woman at the time of ovulation. It becomes easier for the sperm to travel to the egg and helps in fertilization. Intrauterine insemination is less invasive and less expensive compared to IVF. It can be suggested for couples facing:

  • Male infertility
  • Infertility of unknown causes
  • Problems related to ovulation
  • Fertility problems due to cervical mucus
  • Difficulty in conception even after trying.

But IUI is not appropriate for all couples. The fertility specialist will examine both partners before suggesting IUI.

What Determines the Cost of IUI Treatment in Guwahati?

Some factors determine the ultimate cost of IUI treatment. Understanding them will help you prepare better for the fertility journey.

1. Fertility Medications

Some women may require medication to facilitate ovulation and egg production. The medication used can affect the cost of treatment.

2. Diagnostic Tests

There are several tests, including blood tests, ultrasound scans, semen analysis, and other fertility tests that need to be conducted before IUI treatment.

3. Number of IUI Cycles

Since IUI may take more than one cycle to conceive, the couple needs to know the cost of multiple IUI cycles.

4. Individual Fertility Problems

Age, ovarian reserve, sperm quality, ovulation pattern, and other individual fertility factors will determine the cost of treatment.

What Are the Components of the IUI Test Cost in Guwahati?

Before recommending IUI treatment, the fertility specialist will examine both partners to determine their reproductive health status.

These can include some of the following:

Semen Analysis: Checks the number, motility, and morphology of sperm.

Follicular Monitoring: Monitors the follicles in the ovary and determines the right time for insemination.

Hormonal Test: The levels of hormones like AMH, FSH, LH, and TSH are tested when needed.

Ultrasound: Helps in examining the uterus, ovaries, and follicular development.

Fallopian Tube: Tests like HSG may be required to determine if the fallopian tubes are open.

The cost of IUI test in Guwahati is determined based on the investigations that your doctor advises you to undergo. Not all patients need all the investigations.

Which One Should You Opt for – IUI vs. IVF?

Couples compare the cost of IUI treatment in India to the cost of IVF before making their choice. In spite of the same aim to help with conception, procedures differ between these treatments.

With the procedure of IUI, prepared sperm is inserted in the uterus, and fertilization occurs on its own within the body.

In case of IVF, known as In Vitro Fertilization, egg cells are obtained from the ovaries and fertilized with sperm in the laboratory; after that, the embryo is transferred to the uterus.

IVF is usually costlier and more complex than IUI. However, cases that are recommended to undergo IVF treatment include blocked fallopian tubes, severe male-factor infertility, age-related problems with conception, and failed previous treatments.

IVF prices in Guwahati vary widely based on several factors; some clinics publish starting prices of ₹1 lakh or more. The correct choice of treatment depends not only on the cost of it but also on your particular case.
