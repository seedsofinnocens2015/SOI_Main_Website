What Is IUI?

IUI stands for Intrauterine Insemination. It involves putting healthy and washed sperm inside the woman's womb when she is ovulating. The shorter path will increase your chances of getting pregnant. This procedure takes just a few minutes, does not involve any surgical operations, and is virtually pain-free. You can return home right away after it.

Sperm washing: Processing of the semen specimen before the insemination process.

The number of cycles: Usually, 2-3 IUI attempts are needed, so you should consider the total expenses.

How Does IUI Work?

Initial check-up: The doctor takes your history and does basic tests.

Ovulation induction: You might need medicines for maturation of eggs.

Monitoring: Ultrasound checks the size of the eggs.

Collection and washing of sperm: This is done on the day of the procedure.

Insemination: Sperm are injected into the uterus through a thin, flexible tube.

Pregnancy test: Around 14 days post-insemination.

Who Should Choose IUI Treatment?

IUI is recommended in cases where:

  • You have unexplained infertility
  • Your male partner has mild problems with sperm (low sperm count and/or motility)
  • You have problems with ovulation or PCOS
  • You have cervical mucus problems
  • Donor sperm is used
  • Sexual intercourse is not possible for medical or other reasons

IUI tends to work best when one or both fallopian tubes are open, and sperm quality is decent. The doctor can assure you of this through basic tests.

Success Rate for IUI: What Should You Expect?

Here is the truth, IUI is not an assurance of success. It is about 10% to 20% per cycle, depending on your age, sperm quality, and the cause of infertility. Younger women usually have better chances. This is the reason why many doctors recommend trying 3 to 4 cycles of IUI before going to IVF. A professional fertility doctor will advise you according to your personal test results.
