What is an IVF Treatment?

The first question many couples would like to ask is “what is IVF treatment?”

IVF stands for In Vitro Fertilization – an advanced fertility method that involves fertilization of eggs and sperm in a sophisticated laboratory, followed by transferring the embryo into the womb.

Is it true that what is IVF treatment is just an assistance to nature? Absolutely correct; while the fertilization process usually takes place in the fallopian tube, in the case of IVF it happens in the lab before the embryo transfer.

To summarize, what is IVF treatment? It is one of the most effective ART technologies.

    • IVF Treatment Procedure

    Knowing about the entire IVF treatment procedure can ease your fertility treatment process.

    Step 1: Fertility Tests

    The process begins with the consultation process, ultrasound test, hormone level test, sperm test, and fertility diagnosis.

    Step 2: Egg Stimulation Process

    These special drugs help stimulate your ovaries to produce more eggs than just one in your normal ovulation cycle.

    A question arises: how many injections for IVF treatment will you have to undergo? The answer is that the number of injections needed in an IVF treatment procedure totally depends on your personal protocol and usually ranges from 8-12 days of injections every day.

    Step 3: Egg Retrieval

    After the eggs have matured, they are harvested using a simple and minimally invasive procedure done under anesthesia. The procedure is generally painless for many women and usually lasts about 20–30 minutes.

    Step 4: Fertilization

    The harvested eggs are then fertilized using sperm in an advanced embryology lab. The next stage is critical in the IVF process.

    Step 5: Embryo Development

    The embryos are observed for several days by skilled embryologists in order to choose the best embryo to be transferred. Technologies used may also include embryo observation and testing for genetic abnormalities where medically required.

    Step 6: Embryo Transfer

    The embryo that has been selected is carefully transferred to the woman's womb using a fine tube. Embryo transfer is among the easiest and fastest stages of the IVF procedure.

    Step 7: Pregnancy Test

    About two weeks later, the blood test determines whether the implantation was successful.

    All IVF treatments are carefully followed to increase the chances of pregnancy safely.
