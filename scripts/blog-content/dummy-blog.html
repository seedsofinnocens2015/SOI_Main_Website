Many modern IVF centres offer assisted reproductive technologies meant to maximize the chances of pregnancy. Some common fertility treatments are:

    In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

    IVF is basically the process of fertilizing eggs with sperm in a lab setting first, before transferring the embryo into the uterus.

    Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

    IUI is a less invasive fertility treatment, where the prepared sperm is placed directly into the uterus around ovulation.

    Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)

    ICSI is a specialized technique where a single sperm is injected directly into an egg so fertilization is easier.

    Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET)

    FET means previously frozen embryos are thawed, then transferred to the uterus, aiming for better pregnancy outcomes.

    Donor Programs

    Donor egg, donor sperm, and donor embryo programs offer options for patients with specific fertility hurdles. An advanced IVF Centre for Infertility Treatment in Ghaziabad usually provides these treatments under one roof, so care stays continuous and convenient.
