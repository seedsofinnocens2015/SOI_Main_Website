What Does an IVF Doctor Do in Lucknow?

An IVF doctor is a fertility expert who determines the likely cause of infertility in couples and provides the suitable treatment methods for the same. This could be either lifestyle changes, medication, ovulation stimulation, IUI or IVF depending upon the health of the couple.

A good fertility expert does not always start treatment through IVF. They need to know the complete medical history of the couples along with conducting various fertility tests.

According to the treatment guidelines of ICMR, there are various tests which can be conducted for infertility testing like ovulation test, ovarian reserve test, uterine health test, fallopian tube tests, and male factor tests. Age is yet another important factor.

What Should You Ask an IVF Doctor in Lucknow?

Before undergoing treatment, you should ask your doctor these questions:

  1. What are the causes of our infertility?
  2. Do we really need in vitro fertilization or are there other methods?
  3. What tests do both partners have to pass?

  5. How much is our full course of IVF treatment going to cost?

  8. Who will be responsible for our cycle of IVF and embryo transfer?
  9. What are the risks involved?
  10. How many visits should we expect during treatment?

When Do You Need to Consult a Fertility Specialist?

Pregnancy should happen within 12 months of regular and unprotected sex, and if this is not happening, a fertility evaluation is suggested for women aged below 35 years. However, for women above 35 years, this evaluation becomes essential, and for those above 40 years of age, it becomes even more necessary.

It may also become essential to visit an IVF specialist if you experience irregular or absent periods, endometriosis or have had any surgery on your pelvis and ovaries, have blocked fallopian tubes, miscarriages, or your partner suffers from male fertility problems.

Evaluation doesn’t necessarily mean you will be undergoing the IVF process; it only helps in identifying fertility issues and understanding your options.

No. While IVF increases the chance of pregnancy in suitable patients, it does not guarantee pregnancy. Many individual factors influence the outcome of the procedure, such as age, ovarian reserve and egg/sperm factors.

Yes, There may be problems in fertility due to the female partner, the male partner or both partners together. An examination of both partners would allow the doctor to formulate an effective treatment plan.
