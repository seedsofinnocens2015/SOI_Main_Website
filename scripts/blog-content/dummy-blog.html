Fertility and Infertility Defined

Infertility is typically known as the inability to conceive within one year after regular sexual intercourse without contraception. Women over 35 years are advised to see a doctor after six months of failed attempts to conceive.

Some common causes of female infertility include:

  • PCOS
  • Blocked fallopian tubes
  • Endometriosis
  • Ovulation disorders
  • Reduced ovarian reserve
  • Advanced maternal age

In case of male infertility, the reasons could be:

  • Low sperm count
  • Impaired sperm motility
  • Irregular sperm morphology
  • Hormonal imbalance
  • Genetic issues

As infertility is an issue that concerns both men and women, it is necessary to examine both of them before the treatment.

IVF Process Explained

1. Initial Fertility Evaluation

The fertility specialist in delhi NCR evaluates the medical history, previous therapies, hormone reports, ultrasounds, and sperm analysis to tailor a customized plan for each patient.

2. Ovarian Stimulation

Fertility drugs are prescribed to make the ovaries produce many mature eggs as opposed to only one egg in the natural cycle.

3. Follicular Surveillance

Through ultrasounds and blood tests, follicular growth is observed to establish the right moment for egg retrieval.

4. Egg Retrieval

Eggs are retrieved through a minor surgical process that is done under sedation once the follicles mature.

5. Fertilization

Fertilization occurs when the eggs that have been retrieved are combined with healthy sperm in a lab setting. In certain conditions, conventional IVF and ICSI will be suggested.

6. Embryo Development

Embryos are closely observed for a number of days in advanced laboratories to select the best embryo for transfer.

7. Embryo Transfer

The chosen embryo is inserted into the woman’s uterus through a tiny catheter. The procedure is usually painless and does not involve hospitalization.

8. Pregnancy Test

Around two weeks after the embryo transfer, the pregnancy is confirmed through a blood test.
