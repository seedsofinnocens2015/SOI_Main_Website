    When Is IVF the Right Choice?

    Understanding IVF Treatment Step by Step

    When you know the IVF flow, it can reduce a lot of anxiety and unclear fear.

    Step 1: Fertility Assessment

    Reviewing medical history, diagnostic tests, and fertility counselling.

    Step 2: Ovarian Stimulation

    Hormonal injections are used to help multiple eggs mature.

    Step 3: Egg Retrieval

    A minimally invasive procedure done under sedation.

    Step 4: Fertilization

    Eggs and sperm are combined in the IVF laboratory, and if required, ICSI is used.

    Step 5: Embryo Development

    Embryologists track embryo growth for a few days.

    Step 6: Embryo Transfer

    A single healthy embryo is placed into the uterus, using a simple procedure that is usually not very painful.

    Step 7: Pregnancy Test

    About two weeks later, a blood test checks whether the implantation worked.
