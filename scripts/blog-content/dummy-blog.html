What is Laparoscopic Surgery?

Laparoscopic surgery is also known as minimally invasive or keyhole surgery. In laparoscopic surgery, the surgeon conducts the operation through a few cuts instead of conducting one cut at a time.

During laparoscopic surgery in Delhi, a thin device named a laparoscope is introduced inside the body. The laparoscope has a camera, through which the surgeon gets a clear picture of the organs on the monitor. Then, the surgery is conducted through the help of special surgical instruments.

Laparoscopic surgery could provide several benefits for women such as:

  • Small cuts
  • Less pain after the operation
  • Less scars
  • Reduced hospital stays if possible
  • Quick recovery
  • Early return to regular activities

But, the type of surgery should be considered according to the condition of the patient, the health of the patient, the complexity of the process, and other factors evaluated by the surgeon.

When do you need a Laparoscopic Gynecologist?

A skilled laparoscopic gynecologist could examine and operate on a few types of gynecological diseases using minimally invasive surgery if it is clinically appropriate.

The common conditions treated by this type of surgeon are:

Endometriosis

Endometriosis is the growth of tissue similar to the uterine lining outside the uterus. Symptoms of this disease may include severe menstrual pain, chronic pelvic pain, dyspareunia, and fertility issues.

Laparoscopy may be used not only for diagnosis, but also for treatment of endometriosis. The surgeon removes or treats endometriotic lesions and adhesions depending on the degree of severity of the disease.

Ovarian Cysts

There are many types of ovarian cysts, some of which disappear on their own, while others might require surgical treatment. This procedure might be recommended depending on the size, type, symptoms, and features of the cyst.

Uterine Fibroids

Fibroids are non-malignant tumors growing within or near the uterus. The symptoms may be heavy menstruation, pelvic pressure or pain, and fertility issues.

Some of these fibroids may be treated surgically in a minimally invasive way depending on their size and location.

Adenomyosis and Other Pelvic Pathologies

Persistent symptoms associated with adenomyosis and other complicated pelvic diseases may require a more thorough investigation, and in some cases a surgical treatment might be recommended.

Gynaecological Adhesions

Adhesions are bands of scar tissue formed as a result of earlier surgeries or infections. They can be related to pelvic pain among other complications and can be treated through laparoscopic surgeries.
