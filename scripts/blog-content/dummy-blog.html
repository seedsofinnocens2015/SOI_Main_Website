Factors that Affect IVF Treatment Cost in Kanpur

Various factors can determine the total cost of IVF treatment in Kanpur. These factors include:

1. Fertility Tests and Initial Consultation

Before starting the treatment, both the husband and wife may need to undergo various fertility tests such as blood tests, ultrasound, semen tests, and other required tests. The cost of these tests will depend upon the type of tests suggested.

2. Medicines and Injections for IVF

Hormonal medicines and injections are used for the process of ovarian stimulation to promote egg growth in the body. Dose and period of medication vary from person to person and hence affect the cost of the entire treatment process.

3. Egg Retrieval Process

The egg retrieval process takes place once the follicles have developed to a certain stage. Eggs from the ovaries are collected through this process.

4. IVF Lab and Embryology Charges

The lab process is an important step in the IVF process. This includes processes such as fertilization, embryo culture, and monitoring embryo development.

5. Embryo Transfer

Once the embryo growth phase is complete, an appropriate embryo can be chosen for uterine transfer. The number of embryos to be transferred is decided by the fertility specialist based on the needs of the individual patient.

6. Further Fertility Treatments

Some couples might need further treatments such as ICSI, embryo freezing, frozen embryo transfer, or PGD. The use of these procedures could add to the total cost of the IVF process in Kanpur.
