Infertility Is More Common Than You Think

A lot of people assume infertility is a woman's only issue, which is not correct. Actually:

  • About 40% of infertility cases are linked to female factors.
  • Another 40% come from male infertility.
  • The rest can involve both partners together, or unexplained infertility.

    • So fertility specialists usually suggest assessing both partners before treatment even starts, it saves time and removes guesswork.
