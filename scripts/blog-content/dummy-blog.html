What Determines the Cost of IVF Treatment in Meerut?

The cost of IVF in Meerut varies from couple to couple depending on various determinants.

1. Infertility Cause

The cause of infertility plays an important part in determining the course of treatment. There may be many causes of infertility, including female age, ovarian reserve problems, blockage of the fallopian tube, endometriosis, PCOS, male factor infertility, and unexplained infertility.

2. Medication

Hormonal medicines are usually involved in the process of IVF treatment. The type and amount of medicines required by the patient vary a lot.

3. IVF Cycles

Some couples conceive on their first try of IVF, while some may require multiple attempts. Hence, the IVF charges in Meerut may rise due to an increased number of cycles.

4. Other Infertility Procedures

Various other infertility procedures might be recommended by the fertility specialist according to the circumstances of the case.

5. Embryo Freezing and Storage

If there are other available embryos, they can be frozen for future use. The cost of embryo freezing and storage will vary based on the clinic and the duration of storage.

6. Donor Egg IVF

Some women need donor eggs due to decreased ovarian reserve, medical conditions, age factor, or failure in their IVF procedures.

The cost of donor egg IVF procedure in Meerut differs from the cost of standard IVF procedure because it involves some donor processes and medical/lab services.

The fertility expert can help you determine if donor egg IVF is right for you, along with a cost estimate specific to your case.
