What is IUI Treatment?

IUI stands for Intrauterine Insemination. The IUI treatment process involves putting the specially prepared sperm directly into the uterus during ovulation, when an egg is released.

This process normally consists of tracking the egg's release, preparation of the sperm in the lab, and placement of the sperm inside the uterus through a catheter. The process normally takes only a few minutes and normally does not require anesthesia.

What Determines IUI Treatment Cost in Kochi?

Various aspects determine the final cost of IUI treatment in Kochi.

1. Fertility tests: The doctor may opt for hormone tests, ultrasound scans, semen analyses, and tests of openness of at least one fallopian tube.

2. Medicine: Some females may be under drug cover to aid ovulation, but there are those who do not take medication.

3. Cycles of treatments: Each IUI process takes place during an individual menstrual cycle. While some people get pregnant after the first try, other patients may need to go through additional tries.

4. Health state: Such conditions like infertility, difficulties with ovulation, and problems with semen might affect your IUI treatment.

5. Clinic services: Fees for consultation, laboratory, monitoring, and services that may be offered in a package can vary from one fertility clinic to another.

Before proceeding, seek an estimated cost of treatment per cycle and potential costs associated with further treatment from your doctor.

Who Can Be Helped by IUI Therapy?

This treatment may be recommended for couples suffering from some specific fertility issues such as:

  • Mild infertility in men with respect to sperm condition.
  • Some ovulation difficulties that could be treated in a proper way.
  • Unexplained infertility following proper examination.
  • Some cases of donor sperm usage.

It seems to be not the right treatment for all people suffering from certain fertility issues such as blocked fallopian tubes, some abnormal sperm qualities, and age-related ones, and in those cases IVF is better.

Fertility specialist can examine you and make a decision as to which treatment would fit you.
