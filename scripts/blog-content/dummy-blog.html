What Is IUI?

IUI (Intrauterine Insemination) is a very basic fertility procedure. Washed sperm is placed into the uterus around ovulation. This procedure gives the sperm a shorter distance to reach the egg and increases the chances of pregnancy. It takes only 10 to 15 minutes. It is usually painless and does not involve any surgical intervention or hospitalization.

When Should You Consider IUI?

IUI may help in the following cases:

  • Unexplained infertility
  • Male partner has low sperm count or low motility
  • Irregular ovulation or mild PCOS in the woman
  • Cervical mucus problem
  • Donor sperm is being used
  • Difficulty in intercourse due to medical reasons

IUI may not be appropriate in case of blocked fallopian tubes, severe endometriosis or very low sperm count. This will be known through a thorough diagnosis.

Why Choose IUI?

1. Cost-effective: Much cheaper than IVF, making it easy on your budget.

2. Quick and simple: No surgery, no anaesthesia, and you can leave the hospital on the same day.

3. Low dose of medications: Requires lower doses of hormones than IVF.

4. Less stress on the body: Most women get back to their normal life within a short period of time.

5. A good starting point: Even if IUI fails, you can switch to IVF with an excellent plan.

How Does the IUI Procedure Take Place?

1. Consultation and testing: The doctor examines your hormones, ovulation, tubes, and semen.

2. Egg tracking: Ultrasound tests track egg development. Some medicines may be prescribed.

3. Sperm harvesting and preparation: A fresh sample is processed and concentrated in the laboratory.

4. Insemination: The sperm is put into the uterus via a fine, soft catheter.

5. Rest and waiting: You rest for some time and then take the pregnancy test after two weeks.
