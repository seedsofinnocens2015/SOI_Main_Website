What Happens in the First Week of Pregnancy?

The first week of pregnancy begins from the date of your last menstrual period (LMP). However, conception takes place two weeks later. Nonetheless, women looking for 1st Week Pregnancy Symptoms are referring to the first few days of conception or implantation.

At this point, the fertilized egg starts to travel down to the uterus and implantation takes place. It results in hormonal shifts leading to very early pregnancy symptoms 1 week after conception.

1st Week Pregnancy Symptoms

Not every woman will go through the same pregnancy symptoms because every pregnancy is different. The following are some of the common 1st Week Pregnancy Symptoms.

Implantation Bleeding

Light spotting is always one of the early pregnancy symptoms. It happens when the fertilized egg attaches itself to the uterine wall.

Implantation bleeding is characterized by:

  • Pink or brown in color
  • Very light compared to a menstrual period
  • It lasts for just a couple of hours to a maximum of two days

    • Women usually confuse this with discharge 1 week pregnancy symptoms before missed period, and hence it is good to note the color and flow.

    Mild Abdominal Cramping

    Mild cramps can accompany implantation and are considered to be among the 1st Week Pregnancy Symptoms. However, it is milder and does not last long.

    Breast Tenderness

    Breast tenderness is one of the earliest pregnancy symptoms that occur when there are changes in hormone levels. This leads to increased blood flow, causing the breasts to become swollen or tender.

    Fatigue

    Being more tired than usual is one of the earliest pregnancy symptoms. The hormone progesterone starts to rise right after conception, increasing the workload of the body.

    Changes in Vaginal Discharge

    An increase in vaginal discharge is another early sign of pregnancy. If the discharge is clear and odorless and is not associated with itchiness or discomfort, this is considered normal.

    Bloating

    Due to changes in hormones, there are problems with digestion that may lead to bloating. Bloating is one of the common pregnancy symptoms 1 week before a missed period.

    Mood Swings

    Fluctuating hormone levels may cause mood swings. Mood swings are among the most common early pregnancy symptoms.

    Frequent Urination

    Increased urination usually occurs during later stages of pregnancy, but some women have it in the very beginning because of hormonal changes.

    Food Cravings and Nausea from Familiar Smells

    There are certain foods that can become desirable for consumption, while at the same time smells can lead to feelings of nausea. This is the symptom of early pregnancy that many women face.

    Symptoms of Pregnancy During First 72 Hours

    When considering the question of what the first 72 hours of pregnancy symptoms are, it is worth mentioning that during the first three days of pregnancy women experience no visible changes because the process of implantation has not taken place. However, there are certain hormonal changes taking place, and women can experience such symptoms as:

  • Mild fatigue
  • Bloated belly
  • Emotional sensitivity
  • Rise of basal body temperature

    • It should be emphasized that the first 72 hours of pregnancy symptoms are highly individual, and there are cases when women have no symptoms at all.

    Symptoms of Being 3 Days Pregnant

    In case you want to know 3 days pregnant symptoms, it is necessary to mention that the fertilization has just happened. The fertilized egg is still moving towards the uterus.

    3 days pregnant symptoms may include:

  • Bloated belly
  • Fatigue
  • Cervical mucus increase
  • Pelvic pain

    • However, many women experience no symptoms at all.

    How Are Early Pregnancy Symptoms Different from PMS?

    Many symptoms of pregnancy closely resemble premenstrual syndrome (PMS), making it difficult to tell the difference.

    PMS - Signs of Pregnancy

  • Mild cramps - Implantation cramps
  • Mood swings - Mood swings that continue
  • Breast tenderness - Persistent breast tenderness
  • Bloating - Longer-lasting bloating
  • Spotting before periods - Implantation bleeding
  • Fatigue - Increasing fatigue

    • If these signs of pregnancy continue after your expected period, taking a pregnancy test is recommended.

    When Should You Use a Pregnancy Test?

    While some women experience very early signs of pregnancy 1 week post-conception, the accuracy of home pregnancy tests increases after a missed period.

    Tips for accurate results:

  • Use the first morning urine.
  • Wait for a missed period.
  • Follow the instructions provided on the test.
  • See a fertility specialist if you get uncertain results.
