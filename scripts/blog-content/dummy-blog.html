Fertility Problem: More Common Than People Know

Infertility is something that affects lots of couples across the world, but really, it’s much more common than you think. As a rule, infertility is diagnosed when couples attempt conception for a year or more through frequent unprotected sex without results. However, if the woman is older than 35 years old, then a fertility check is often recommended after six months of trying. Besides, infertility is not an exclusively "woman's problem." In fact, sometimes both male and female factors are equally responsible, and thus, it is important to have a full fertility investigation done for accurate diagnosis and correct treatment. The common reasons for infertility may include:

  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)
  • Endometriosis
  • Blockage of the fallopian tubes
  • Ovulation disorder
  • Ovarian reserve problems
  • Low sperm count or bad sperm quality
  • Hormonal issues
  • Genetic conditions
  • Unexplained infertility

    • And so, the first step towards successful treatment is discovering the cause, which is the purpose of fertility investigations.

    All-in-one Infertility Solutions for You

    Reproductive medicine today offers several solutions depending on the causes of infertility. At Seeds of Innocens, each treatment always starts with thorough fertility testing, so that the plan fits the diagnosis, and not vice versa.

    In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

    In vitro fertilization is one of the most sophisticated assisted reproduction procedures. During this procedure, eggs and sperm are fertilized in laboratory conditions, then a high-quality embryo is chosen and inserted into the uterus in order to increase the probability of pregnancy.

    Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)

    Intracytoplasmic sperm injection may be particularly effective in cases of severe male infertility. In this procedure, one sperm cell is inserted into a mature egg in order to increase fertilization.

    Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

    Intrauterine insemination is a rather non-invasive procedure. This method includes the insertion of specially processed sperm into the uterus at the time of ovulation.

    Frozen Embryo Transfer (FET)

    Frozen embryos can be used by couples to conceive. It increases the possibilities of successful conception through Frozen Embryo Transfer.

    Fertility Preservation

    If you plan to have children later in life or if your current treatments might interfere with your fertility, then fertility preservation becomes one of the most valuable procedures for you. Egg freezing, sperm freezing, and embryo preservation preserve your reproductive capability for the future.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. When should I see a fertility specialist?

    If you are under 35 years old and unable to conceive after one year of trying, or above 35 years old and struggling to conceive for 6 months, then it is recommended that you seek a fertility assessment.

    2. Are there other alternatives to IVF for treating infertility?

    It depends. Based on the diagnosis, ovulation induction, IUI, ICSI, fertility preservation, or even some life changes could be suggested.

    3. Can male infertility be cured?

    Yes. In most cases, it can be treated using various methods such as drugs, life changes, surgery, or even using ART like ICSI.
