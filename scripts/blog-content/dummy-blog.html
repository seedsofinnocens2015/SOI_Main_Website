What Is IUI Treatment?

IUI or Intrauterine Insemination is a fertility treatment wherein healthy and concentrated sperm is inserted into the uterus of the woman during the ovulation period. This increases the chances of conception as the journey of the sperm towards the egg is shortened. This procedure can be considered as giving the sperm a "head start". It is a quick procedure taking only 5 to 10 minutes and does not require any anesthesia.

Who Should Try IUI?

Following people should try IUI:

If you are suffering from unexplained infertility

In case the Husband is having slight male factor problem such as low sperm count or poor sperm motility

In case wife has irregular ovulation or mild PCOD

If there is an issue with cervical mucus

If there is use of Donor Sperm

If intercourse is difficult because of any physical reason.

IUI Success Rate: What You Should Know

Honesty is the key: The success rate of IUI is generally around 10% to 20% per cycle based on age, sperm quality and the cause of infertility. Women below 35 years old achieve better success rates. Success increases through proper scheduling of ovulation and proper medical supervision, that's why choosing a good clinic is so crucial.
