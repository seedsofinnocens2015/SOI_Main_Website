What Is IVF?

In vitro fertilization (IVF) refers to the process through which eggs and sperm are fertilized to form an embryo in a laboratory before being transferred into the uterus.

In vitro fertilization is usually recommended in cases where couples face issues like blocked fallopian tubes, male factor infertility, ovulatory issues, unexplained infertility, endometriosis, or have failed to conceive with the help of other fertility therapies.

What is the Typical IVF Price in Kolkata?

The IVF price in Kolkata usually starts from ₹1 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh or even more, per cycle, depending on your treatment course and needs.

However, you should understand that the IVF package price might not always cover all expenses. You may be additionally billed for medicines, tests, extra lab work, egg storage, or any donor services.

Thus, when comparing IVF prices in Kolkata, couples should get a clear quote that includes all expenses of their treatment cycle.

Factors that Could Determine the Cost of an IVF Procedure in Kolkata

There could be several aspects that will determine the final cost of your IVF treatment in Kolkata, which may include the following:

1. Fertility drugs:

Injectable hormones along with other medicines can account for a huge portion of the total cost of treatment.

2. Tests and diagnosis:

Blood tests, ultrasonography, semen analysis, and others might be needed during your treatment process.

3. Procedures conducted in the lab:

Egg retrieval, fertilization, culture of embryo, and embryo transfer are the procedures that may add up to the cost of treatment.

4. Embryo cryopreservation:

If any of the extra embryos can be frozen and stored, the cost of the procedure will go up.

5. ICSI:

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection will be needed in some cases, which will increase the total cost of your treatment.

6. IVF cycles:

More than one cycle may be needed to conceive.

What All Will Be In The Cost Of IVF Treatment?

It is important for you to know what is included in the IVF cost in Kolkata that is being quoted by the fertility clinic. A typical IVF treatment may include:

  • Consultation with a fertility specialist
  • Fertility testing and diagnostics
  • Stimulation of ovaries
  • Monitoring through ultrasound and blood tests
  • Collection of eggs from ovaries
  • Collection of sperm
  • Fertilization process in lab
  • Culture and monitoring of embryos
  • Embryo transfer

But inclusions may differ from clinic to clinic. Some medicines, advanced laboratory procedures, donor services and embryo freezing may be charged extra.
