What Are Covered in the Cost of IVF Treatment in Haldwani?

When comparing IVF treatment costs in Haldwani, do not take the ivf charges in haldwani quote alone. Request the clinic for a detailed list of what makes up the total cost of the procedure.

The cost of IVF in Haldwani includes:

1. First consultation:

The fertility expert will discuss your medical history and suggest relevant tests.

2. Diagnosis:

Blood tests, hormonal tests, semen analysis, and ultrasound scans are done to check for fertility.

3. Medicines for ovarian stimulation:

Medicines to stimulate your ovaries to produce eggs.

4. Egg retrieval:

Extraction of mature eggs through a procedure.

5. Fertilization in the lab:

Fertilization of the eggs with sperm in the laboratory.

6. Embryo transfer:

Transferring an embryo into the uterus.

7. Post-treatment care:

Your doctor may prescribe medicines and ask for a pregnancy test.

Not every clinic provides all these services in its package deal. Additional medicines, tests, anaesthetic, freezing of the embryos, etc. may have to be paid for as separate costs.

Ask your doctor to provide a clear breakdown in advance so that you will know what exactly should be paid for separately.

Factors That Influence IVF Treatment Cost in Haldwani

Each couple facing fertility problems is different, thus, IVF treatment cost in Haldwani is individualised and unique to each specific case. The following factors may affect the total costs.

1. Woman's Age and Her Overall Fertility Status

Age, number of mature eggs, ovarian reserve, egg quality, etc. in some cases can affect the IVF protocol chosen. In some cases the patient might need additional tests or different stimulating protocol.

2. Cost of Fertility Drugs

Fertility drugs needed for ovarian stimulation can make up a big part of the whole cost. They differ according to the dose and period prescribed by the doctor individually for you.

3. Additional Procedure ICSI Required

It is a process when the single sperm is injected directly into an egg.

4. Embryo Banking and Pre-Implantation Genetic Screening Tests

When appropriate embryos are available for later use, embryo freezing may be recommended. In selected situations, preimplantation genetic screening test may also be recommended. This procedure will add to the cost of the total procedure and is not necessary for all patients.

5. Number of Cycles of Fertility Treatment

IVF does not assure conception in one single cycle itself. Couples may require additional embryo transfer cycle in case of failed cycles of treatment.

How Much Does IVF with Donor Eggs in Haldwani Cost?

There are cases where it would be not be ideal for the woman to go ahead with her own egg for treatment of infertility through IVF. Donor egg IVF procedure may be discussed in the following situations: when a person has low ovarian reserve, early menopause, certain genetic disorders, and recurrent failure of treatment.

The egg from the donor is then fertilized in a lab by using the sperms and the resulting embryo is transferred to the uterus of the recipient mother in case she is medically eligible to conceive.

In Haldwani, the donor egg ivf cost in haldwani may even exceed that of a regular IVF process. The published costs of donor egg IVF in India are roughly ₹2.5 lakh – ₹3.8 lakh. Nonetheless, the cost in Haldwani may vary from that figure.

Total cost will depend on:

  • Cost of the donor testing and medical tests involved
  • Cost of fertilisation and lab work
  • Cost of medicines required for the prospective mother
  • Cost of embryo transfer and additional treatments
  • Extra costs, if needed medically

A donor egg IVF is not necessary for all the patients. Your reproductive health expert will give you more information to help you understand your options and make an informed choice.

Is IVF a Good Choice for Couples Suffering from Infertility?

The inability to conceive can be emotionally draining. Couples can feel uncertain, stressed, and under pressure from their family members and society. Nevertheless, being unable to conceive is a health problem rather than a personal one. There are a number of factors which make couples eligible for IVF such as blocked fallopian tubes, some sperm fertility issues, some cases of unexplained infertility and even failed treatments before. The type of treatment depends on the diagnosis. If you have been trying to conceive for 12 months and haven't succeeded or for 6 months if the woman is 35 years old and above, you should consider getting a fertility check-up. Even earlier check-up is possible if you have irregular menstrual cycles or suffer from any reproductive disorders. IVF is not a guarantee of pregnancy but the fertility check-up will give you more information about your situation and help you choose further treatment options.
