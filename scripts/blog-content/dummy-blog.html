When Should You See an Infertility Specialist?

Many couples put off fertility consultations because they think they need to try for many months before visiting a fertility specialist.

Couples may be ready to seek a fertility evaluation after one year of having regular unprotected sex without conceiving. In case you are aged 35 or older, the fertility evaluation is often recommended after six months.

However, you can choose to visit a fertility specialist in Guwahati early if you have any of the following conditions: irregular periods, previous miscarriages, PCOS or endometriosis, past pelvic surgery, tubal factors, or a male infertility problem.

Visiting a fertility specialist early does not automatically mean that you will need IVF. The only thing it means is early identification of problems.
