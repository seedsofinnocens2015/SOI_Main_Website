When to Visit an IVF Specialist?

Most couples wait a lot of time before going to see a fertility doctor.

As a general rule, a fertility check-up is advisable for couples trying to conceive for at least one year of regularly having unprotected intercourse and not getting pregnant if the woman is younger than 35.

If the woman is 35 or older, a check-up after six months of trying should be done.

You might want to visit a fertility doctor in Faridabad sooner if you experience irregular periods, have reproductive problems, have undergone pelvic surgery, have miscarriages, or have other fertility issues.

There is no need to wait if you know for sure that you have fertility issues.

Is IVF Always Successful?

The very first thing that one needs to know when planning to go through the process of IVF is the fact that no doctor can ensure pregnancy.

IVF Results Are Always Variable from Person to Person

Various factors such as age, quality of eggs and sperm, embryo growth and development, reproductive health, and the actual reason behind the infertility may affect the result of IVF.

Thus, you should be careful when choosing an IVF clinic, especially if it promises you a guaranteed pregnancy or a 100% IVF success rate. Instead of relying on such false promises, you need to ask your doctor about your personal situation and various factors affecting the success. The right thing to do is to choose a doctor who will offer you medical consultation rather than making unrealistic promises.

An IVF specialist finds the causes of fertility disorders and suggests appropriate treatment methods, which can vary from medicines to IUI and IVF.

5. When Should I Visit an Infertility Specialist?

An evaluation of infertility is usually required if pregnancy has not been achieved within 12 months of trying when the female is below 35 years old. Evaluation of infertility is advised within six months if the female is 35 or older. If you are aware of any fertility problems, then assessment can be done even before six months.
