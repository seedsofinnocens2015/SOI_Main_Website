Cervix Meaning in Hindi (सर्विक्स मीनिंग इन हिंदी)

The cervix is known as Garbhashaya Greeva in Hindi. In simple language, it can be explained as the lower part or the "mouth" of the womb. The cervix, uterus, and vagina are parts of the female reproductive system and are interconnected.

"Cervix meaning in Hindi" is one of the phrases people will find online when they search for the definition of cervix.

Simply stated, Cervix = Garbhashaya Greeva = the lower end of the womb.

During the menstrual cycle, pregnancy, and childbirth, the cervix serves very distinctive and significant purposes.

What Is Cervix? (Cervix kya hota hai)

This cervix is the structure that acts as a passage from the uterus to the vagina. During menstruation, menstrual blood passes from the uterus to the vagina via the cervix. Likewise, the cervix helps in making the pathway easy for the sperm to pass into the uterus during conception.

During pregnancy, the cervix usually remains closed and firm to keep the baby inside the uterus.

As delivery becomes closer, there will be changes in the cervix, which include becoming softer, thinner, and dilated.

What is the importance of cervix in a pregnancy?

Knowledge about cervix when pregnant is crucial because length and position of the cervix are often assessed with the help of an ultrasound during pregnancy.

Normally, in a healthy pregnancy, the cervix should be closed and long enough until the end of the pregnancy. As delivery is getting closer, changes begin to occur with the cervix.

However, sometimes in some pregnancies, the cervix becomes too short or weak too soon; in this case, more monitoring or treatment is required.

Thus, the mentioning of the cervical length in an ultrasound report does not mean that there is any issue with it. The cervical length has to be evaluated based on gestational age, symptoms and clinical factors.

What Does Cervical Length Mean?

The cervical length is the length of the cervix (the lower portion of the uterus).

The cervical length meaning in hindi is the indication of the length of the cervix. In pregnancy, the cervical length is determined very precisely by an ultrasound, more specifically, a transvaginal ultrasound.

Why is cervical length measured? (cervical length meaning in hindi)

The reason why the cervical length is important to know during pregnancy is that the cervix can get shorter too soon,

When the cervix becomes shorter at an early time, there is an increased risk for preterm delivery. Nevertheless, it should be noted that the course of pregnancy is not defined only by the length of the cervix; there is an evaluation carried out by taking into account the general condition of the pregnancy.

What does "Cervix Measured" mean?

When "cervix measured" occurs in your ultrasound report, it usually means that your doctor or sonographer measured your cervix length or position when you were having the ultrasound. It can be one of the ways of monitoring your pregnancy. There are some cases where the measurement of the cervical length becomes especially important for your doctor.

Instead of interpreting the results yourself depending on the particular cervical length value from the report, it is better to discuss the results with your gynecologist taking into account your gestation period.

Normal changes occur in the cervix as pregnancy advances. Normally, when pregnant, the cervix assists in keeping the baby in the uterus. The cervix gradually changes to become soft and open in readiness for delivery.

In case your doctor finds that your cervix is short, he/she will explain the options depending on the circumstances. This means that the report on cervical length should always be taken in consideration of your pregnancy's age and medical history.

How does the cervix relate to fertility?

The cervix is an important part of the fertility process. The cervical mucus normally changes during the ovulation period to assist the sperm in moving.

Fertility cannot only depend on the cervix alone. There are many factors such as egg quality, ovulation, the state of the fallopian tubes, sperm, age, among other factors that affect conception.

In case you have been unable to conceive for a long time, a fertility assessment would be recommended.

During IVF (In Vitro Fertilization), the fertilization process happens in a laboratory. After that, the embryo is put inside the uterus. The insertion of the embryo inside the uterus is done through the cervix using a catheter.

Hence, the structure of the cervix and its accessibility become significant during the process of IVF.
