Ovulation Explained

Ovulation is one of the phases of the menstrual cycle when under the influence of hormones, one of the ovaries releases a mature egg. It passes into the fallopian tubes and can be fertilized.

When there is no fertilization, the egg dies and menstruation begins.

As pregnancy can only happen in case of the egg release, women wonder whether you can conceive without ovulating. Yes, conception takes place because of ovulation, either natural or stimulated by fertility therapy.

Is It Possible to Conceive Without Ovulating?

The answer to the question whether it is possible to conceive without ovulating is negative in terms of the natural menstrual cycle.

Without ovulation:

  • There is no egg release.
  • Fertilization cannot take place.

    • Medical Problems Preventing Ovulation

    Various medical conditions can lead to lack of ovulation, such as:

    Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)

    PCOS is the most prevalent cause of anovulation. The hormonal imbalance does not allow your eggs to develop normally.

    Thyroid Disease

    Either hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism can prevent ovulation.

    Hormonal Imbalance

    Abnormal amounts of prolactin, FSH, LH, or estrogen may affect the egg release.

    Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (POI)

    Some women become infertile at the age less than 40.

    High Levels of Stress

    Constant stress influences the production of hormones and results in the temporary absence of ovulation.

    Overweight and Underweight Body Condition
