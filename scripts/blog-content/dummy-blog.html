What is a False Positive Pregnancy Test?

A false positive pregnancy test is a situation where a pregnancy test yields a positive result even though you are not pregnant. This can be quite confusing and overwhelming, especially if you have done a home pregnancy test kit positive or pregnancy kit positive.

It is, however, crucial to understand can you get a false positive pregnancy test? The answer is yes, although it is quite rare and may occur for some medical or technical reason.
