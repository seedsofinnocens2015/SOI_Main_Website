One of them is test tube baby. Even though this word might sound difficult to understand, the actual meaning of it is quite simple – a test tube baby is a baby conceived using the in vitro fertilisation procedure, during which an egg and sperm are united to conceive an embryo that will then be put inside the womb of a woman. At present, IVF has become a sophisticated and very popular method of fertility treatment used in various types of infertility cases. If you are trying to find out what a test tube baby is, how the test tube baby process works, or the test tube baby cost, learning about the procedure will surely help you make your decisions.

What Is a Test Tube Baby?

A test tube baby is a baby born as a result of a procedure of fertility treatment in which fertilization happens outside the body in a laboratory setting. This process is called in vitro fertilization or IVF.

IVF is a procedure during which mature oocytes are extracted from the woman and fertilised using sperm in a special laboratory. After that, the embryo is placed into the uterus. If implantation succeeds, then further pregnancy proceeds naturally. The term “test tube baby” is often used in colloquial language; however, specialists working in the sphere of fertility treatments prefer to use the term IVF since the procedure requires complex laboratory equipment instead of putting the egg and sperm in the test tube.

What Is the Test Tube Baby Technique Called?

The test tube baby technique is known as IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation). "In vitro" stands for "in glass," and it refers to the laboratory conditions under which fertilisation occurs.

Depending on the fertility status of the couple, doctors can also suggest additional procedures such as ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection), cryopreservation of embryos, blastocyst culture, or genetic testing of embryos.

Test Tube Baby Process: Step by Step

A test tube baby process normally consists of a number of steps. The exact treatment regimen may vary depending on various factors, including the woman's age, ovarian reserve, sperm status, her medical history, etc.

1. Fertility Evaluation

Before beginning the process of IVF, there will be fertility tests done for the couple. These will consist of hormonal studies, ultrasound, tests for ovarian reserve, semen analysis, and other investigations as required.

2. Ovarian Stimulation

There will be a prescription of fertility drugs that will enable the growth of multiple follicles by stimulating the ovaries. This is done by monitoring with ultrasound and, at times, blood test too.

3. Egg Collection

Once the follicles have reached the appropriate stage, a medication is given and the mature eggs are extracted from the body in a process called egg collection.

4. Fertilization in Lab

After collecting the eggs, these are exposed to the sperm that is prepared in the IVF lab. Sometimes, it is suggested to do ICSI, wherein a single sperm is injected into an egg to facilitate fertilization.

5. Development of Embryos

The fertilized eggs are closely watched by the embryologists to see how they are developing as embryos.

6. Embryo Transfer

The chosen embryo is transferred into the womb via a flexible tube called a catheter. The process is typically fast and rarely needs surgical intervention.

7. Pregnancy Test

After the necessary time interval, a blood test is taken to determine if the implantation led to pregnancy.

Test-Tube Babies: Who Should Consider IVF?

IVF is usually suggested to couples dealing with various reasons of infertility. These include blocked or scarred fallopian tubes, ovulation disorders, diminished ovarian reserve, endometriosis, male infertility issues, idiopathic infertility, or age-related infertility. IVF can also be recommended to people if other infertility treatments failed to help with pregnancy or some reproductive conditions require reproductive technology. Notably, IVF is not necessarily the right solution for everybody. A fertility doctor must evaluate a person or a couple prior to suggesting any treatment methods.

IVF and Test Tube Baby Difference

Many people search for the difference between IVF and test tube baby, but medically, there is no separate treatment called “test tube baby.The term test tube baby is a popular expression used to describe a baby conceived through IVF. IVF is the actual medical procedure. In simple terms:

Test Tube Baby IVF
Common or popular term Medical term
Refers to a baby conceived through assisted reproduction Refers to the fertility treatment
Not a separate procedure Involves fertilisation in a laboratory
Often used in everyday searches Used by fertility specialists

Thus, test tube baby vs IVF is actually not an issue because a test tube baby is actually the outcome of IVF treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does the term test-tube baby refer to the IVF baby?

Yes. A test-tube baby can be referred to as an IVF baby. While test-tube baby is a common name, the actual treatment procedure is called In-vitro Fertilization (IVF).

2. Is IVF procedure painful?

Certain steps like ovarian stimulation and egg retrieval might cause some discomfort. The entire process will be made easy with proper medications.

3. Is IVF sure to make one pregnant?

No treatment can ensure pregnancy because this result is dependent on several individual factors like age, quality of eggs and sperm, embryonic development, and infertility issues.

4. What is the duration of the test tube baby procedure?

A cycle of IVF typically requires several weeks from the very beginning up to the embryo transfer and pregnancy test, although the time period is different for each case.

5. Is IVF applicable for men’s infertility?

Yes, IVF, especially in combination with ICSI, can be used in some cases of male infertility.
