Understanding Age 35+ Fertility

Age 35+ Fertility is one of the major concerns for women planning to conceive in the future. With the increase in age, there is a gradual decrease in natural fertility due to the decrease in the quantity and quality of eggs. This has led many women to look for ways of improving fertility after 35.

Though there is a decrease in fertility with age, conception is still possible with the right guidance and Infertility treatment.

Fertility After 35: What Changes?

In the case of fertility after 35, several changes are noted:

  • Decrease in the quantity of eggs available (ovarian reserve)
  • Decrease in the quality of the eggs available
  • Increase in the incidence of chromosomal abnormalities in the eggs
  • Alterations in hormonal balance and ovulation cycles

    • It is not impossible to pregnancy after 35, and with proper planning and guidance, the chances of success are greatly improved.
