✣OUR SERVICES

My practice supports a wide range
of fertility needs including:

IUI, IVF & ICSI

Personalised fertility treatments designed around your diagnosis, cycle response, and parenthood goals.

Genetic Testing

PGT-A and PGT-M screening support to help identify healthy embryos before transfer.

Blastocyst Transfer

Advanced embryo transfer planning at the blastocyst stage for carefully selected IVF cycles.

Cryopreservation

Egg, sperm, and embryo freezing options for future fertility preservation and treatment flexibility.

PRP & Ovarian Rejuvenation

Supportive regenerative options for selected patients seeking ovarian function and fertility care.

TESA/PESA

Surgical sperm retrieval options for male factor infertility and advanced assisted reproduction cycles.

Donor Program

Guidance for donor egg, donor sperm, and donor embryo pathways with ethical counselling support.

Surrogacy

Coordinated surrogacy guidance with medical planning, counselling, and treatment cycle support.

Genetic Counselling

Clear counselling for inherited conditions, embryo testing choices, and family planning decisions.

Laparoscopy & Hysteroscopy

Minimally invasive procedures to evaluate and treat uterine, tubal, and pelvic fertility concerns.

✣WHO I HELP

I specialize in helping...

  • ✓Couples who have gone through failed IVF cycles;
  • ✓Women with low AMH and reduced ovarian reserve;
  • ✓Couples navigating difficult fertility journeys with advanced genetic testing;
  • ✓Patients seeking personalised IVF, ICSI, or donor programme guidance;
  • ✓Women with recurrent pregnancy loss or repeated implantation failure;
  • ✓Couples who need compassionate clarity before choosing their next fertility step.
